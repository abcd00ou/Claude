# Weekly Cadence

**Purpose:** Make the marketing team operate like a revenue intelligence desk.

---

## Monday — Signal Refresh

- Read new entries from Claude orchestrator, DRAM, storage, end market, ASIC, chip maker, and foundry agents.
- Update `money_signals.md`.
- Mark signals as `new`, `active`, `watch`, or `expired`.

## Tuesday — Account Mapping

- Update `revenue_board.md`.
- Re-rank top 20 accounts.
- Assign `Attack`, `Defend`, `Expand`, or `Watch`.
- Identify top 10 actions for the week.

## Wednesday — Asset Production

- Create or refresh account briefs.
- Update product pain messaging.
- Build battlecards and proof sheets for active campaigns.
- Send draft assets to sales and FAE owners.

## Thursday — Sales Review

- Review account actions with sales/product/FAE.
- Confirm customer-ready claims and remove weak claims.
- Decide pricing/mix recommendation changes.
- Assign owner and deadline for every customer action.

## Friday — KPI And Decision Review

- Update `kpi_tracker.md`.
- Record meetings, pipeline, qualification starts, and campaign movement.
- Kill or downgrade low-yield plays.
- Publish the weekly memory revenue memo:
  - Top 10 money signals.
  - Top 10 account actions.
  - Pricing/mix changes.
  - Assets shipped.
  - Risks and escalations.
