# Product-To-Pain Matrix

**Purpose:** Turn technical memory products into account-specific buying pain, proof, pricing/mix action, and KPI.

---

## Matrix

| Product | Customer Pain | Best-Fit Accounts | Value Message | Proof Required | Pricing / Mix Lever | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HBM3E / HBM4 | GPU/ASIC ramps are blocked by bandwidth, power, and qualified supply | NVIDIA, AMD, AWS, Google, Meta, Microsoft | Secure bandwidth supply before platform ramp locks; reduce qualification and allocation risk | Qualification status, thermal/electrical validation, roadmap samples, supply allocation view | Premium pricing, LTA, roadmap lock-in, bundled DDR5/SSD attach | HBM design wins, LTA value, allocation share |
| HBM4E Roadmap | Customers need 2027 platform certainty before 2026 architecture freeze | NVIDIA, AMD, custom ASIC teams | Commit early to avoid 2027 bottleneck and align package/base-die roadmap | Sample timing, package compatibility, validation milestones | Pre-commit allocation, joint roadmap workshop | Roadmap engagements, pre-LTA pipeline |
| DDR5 RDIMM | CPU-side inference and AI server refresh require density and bandwidth | Dell, HPE, Supermicro, ODMs, enterprise AI | Increase inference throughput and server utilization without architecture change | Server QVL, reliability data, power/performance proof | Attach-rate expansion, mix-up to higher density | DDR5 attach rate, density mix, OEM design-ins |
| MRDIMM | Memory bandwidth limits CPU-side AI and data preprocessing | Enterprise AI, OEMs, cloud CPU inference | Higher bandwidth per socket improves inference and data pipeline efficiency | Benchmark versus RDIMM, platform support | Premium mix shift | MRDIMM pipeline, platform qualifications |
| Enterprise SSD / QLC | AI retrieval, checkpointing, and dataset storage costs are rising | Hyperscalers, neo-clouds, OEMs | Lower TCO per usable PB while preserving endurance and latency | Workload endurance data, read latency, power/TB | Shift from price/GB to TCO, power, endurance | Enterprise SSD revenue, QLC mix, gross margin |

| CXL Memory | Inference and RAG workloads need memory expansion beyond local DRAM | OEMs, enterprise AI, hyperscaler platform teams | Expand memory capacity and improve utilization without overbuying CPU servers | Platform compatibility, latency, pooling proof | New category attach, solution bundle | CXL pilots, platform qualifications |

---

## Positioning Rules

- Do not sell HBM as a commodity component; sell supply certainty, platform risk reduction, and roadmap alignment.
- Do not sell enterprise SSD on price/GB when NAND is tight; sell cost per successful AI retrieval/checkpoint and power per PB.
- Attach DDR5/MRDIMM and enterprise SSD to every HBM or GPU/ASIC account conversation.
- Use CXL as a future-proofing message for memory-bound inference, not as a replacement for HBM.
