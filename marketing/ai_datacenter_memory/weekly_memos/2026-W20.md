## Executive Summary

This week should focus on five revenue moves:

1. Launch the Microsoft/Azure AI memory bottleneck brief.
2. Launch AWS Trainium3 memory attach and AI SSD TCO pitch.
3. Build Google Ironwood inference memory roadmap session.
4. Prepare NVIDIA Rubin HBM4/HBM4E qualification proof pack.
5. Push OEM AI server attach-rate plays for Supermicro, Dell, and HPE.

The strongest commercial logic is simple: AI infrastructure is now constrained by memory, storage, packaging, and grid deployment timing, not just accelerator silicon. Customers with aggressive capex and platform ramps will pay for qualified supply certainty, validated roadmaps, and lower deployment risk.

## Pricing And Mix Guidance

- HBM: protect premium positioning; prefer allocation/LTA framing over discounting.
- DDR5/MRDIMM: push higher-density attach on AI server BOMs; package with enterprise SSD where possible.
- Enterprise SSD/QLC: avoid price/GB-only messaging; lead with power/TB, endurance, retrieval economics, and qualification.
- CXL: position as 2026-2027 roadmap engagement for memory-bound inference, not as near-term HBM replacement.

---

## Sales Meeting Ask

Ask each account team for:

1. Current incumbent memory/storage supplier.
2. Next platform qualification date.
3. Whether customer is discussing HBM allocation, DDR5 density, enterprise SSD capacity, or CXL roadmap.
4. One customer pain statement that can be validated in the next meeting.
5. Pipeline estimate or design-win value if the play works.
