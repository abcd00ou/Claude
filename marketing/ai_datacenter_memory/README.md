## Mission

Turn AI supply-chain intelligence into revenue actions: which customer, which product, what urgency, what price/mix, what proof, and what sales motion will create revenue now or secure future allocation.

This is not a research archive. Every entry must end in one of four commercial actions:

- `Attack`: pursue displacement or new design-in.
- `Defend`: protect existing share, allocation, or roadmap position.
- `Expand`: attach additional memory/storage products to an existing account.
- `Watch`: monitor until a trigger makes the account actionable.

## Agent Inputs

- DRAM agent: HBM allocation, DDR5, supplier capacity, pricing, qualification status.
- Storage agent: enterprise SSD, QLC, KV SSD, AI storage tiers.
- End Market agent: hyperscaler capex, cloud backlog, neo-cloud and sovereign AI demand.
- ASIC / Chip Maker agents: GPU and custom ASIC platform ramps.
- Foundry agent: CoWoS, base-die, packaging constraints.
- Analysis agent: demand models, supply-gap interpretation, pricing-power logic.
- Orchestrator: cross-segment signals and account-level synthesis.
