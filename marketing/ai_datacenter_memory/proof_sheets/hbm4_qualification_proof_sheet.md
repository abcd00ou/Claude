# Proof Sheet - HBM4 / HBM4E Qualification

**Audience:** Accelerator vendors, hyperscalers, FAE, product line  
**Goal:** Prove readiness for Rubin, MI-series, and custom ASIC roadmap discussions.

## Required Proof

- Product generation: HBM4 / HBM4E.
- Stack height and capacity options.
- Bandwidth per stack.
- Power and thermal envelope.
- Base die/package compatibility.
- Customer qualification status.
- Sample availability date.
- Mass production availability date.
- Supply allocation scenario.
- Reliability and test coverage.

## Claims That Need Approval

- Any customer-specific qualification claim.
- Any competitor comparison.
- Any supply allocation promise.
- Any roadmap date not already approved by product line.

## Customer Ask

Request a joint qualification milestone review with product, FAE, and procurement decision-makers.
