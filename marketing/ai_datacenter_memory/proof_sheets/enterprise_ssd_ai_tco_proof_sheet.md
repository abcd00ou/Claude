# Proof Sheet - Enterprise SSD / AI Retrieval TCO

**Audience:** Hyperscalers, neo-clouds, enterprise AI infrastructure teams  
**Goal:** Shift decision from price per GB to cost per AI retrieval/checkpoint.

## Required Proof

- Capacity options.
- Sequential and random read performance.
- Write endurance.
- Power per TB.
- Failure rate / reliability claim.
- Thermal profile.
- Workload benchmark for retrieval or checkpointing.
- TCO comparison.
- Platform qualification status.

## Customer Ask

Request a workload TCO review using the customer's retrieval, checkpoint, or model-loading assumptions.
