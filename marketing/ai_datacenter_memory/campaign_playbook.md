# Campaign Playbook

**Purpose:** Convert money signals into campaigns that sales can use within one week.

---

## Campaign 1 — AI Memory Bottleneck Executive Brief

**Trigger:** HBM sold out, hyperscaler capex rising, platform ramp approaching.  
**Target:** Microsoft, AWS, Google, Meta, Oracle, CoreWeave.  
**Products:** HBM4/HBM4E, DDR5/MRDIMM, enterprise SSD.  
**Message:** AI infrastructure plans fail when memory allocation, qualification, and storage attach are handled too late.  
**Offer:** Executive roadmap session + LTA/allocation proposal + platform BOM review.  
**Assets:** Account brief, HBM allocation one-pager, DDR5/SSD attach calculator.  
**KPI:** Executive meetings, LTA pipeline, attached DDR5/SSD revenue.

---

## Campaign 2 — Custom ASIC Memory Attach

**Trigger:** Trainium, TPU, MTIA, Maia, Broadcom/Marvell custom ASIC updates.  
**Target:** AWS, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Broadcom, Marvell.  
**Products:** HBM, DDR5, CXL, AI SSD.  
**Message:** Custom ASICs reduce some merchant GPU dependency but increase direct HBM, memory bandwidth, and retrieval-storage pressure.  
**Offer:** ASIC memory roadmap alignment workshop.  
**Assets:** HBM/CXL architecture map, AI SSD workload brief, qualification checklist.  
**KPI:** Roadmap workshops, design-in opportunities, qualification starts.

---

## Campaign 3 — Enterprise AI Server Attach Rate

**Trigger:** OEM/ODM AI server ramp, enterprise inference growth.  
**Target:** Dell, HPE, Supermicro, Quanta, Wiwynn, Foxconn.  
**Products:** DDR5 RDIMM, MRDIMM, enterprise SSD, CXL.  
**Message:** AI server revenue is won by qualified, high-density memory and storage BOMs before customer platform lock.  
**Offer:** Pre-qualified SKU stack and AI server BOM optimization.  
**Assets:** OEM battlecard, QVL checklist, TCO calculator.  
**KPI:** OEM design-ins, attach rate, density mix.

---

## Campaign 4 — AI Retrieval Storage TCO

**Trigger:** Enterprise SSD pricing rises, agentic AI workload growth, vector DB/RAG adoption.  
**Target:** Hyperscalers, neo-clouds, enterprise AI adopters.  
**Products:** Enterprise SSD, QLC, KV SSD.  
**Message:** AI storage should be bought by retrieval economics, not only price/GB.  
**Offer:** Workload TCO and benchmark review.  
**Assets:** Retrieval TCO calculator, endurance proof, power/TB sheet.  
**KPI:** Enterprise SSD pipeline, QLC mix, KV SSD pilots.

---

## Campaign 5 — Competitor Weakness Fast-Switch Kit

**Trigger:** Competitor HBM qualification delay, SSD quality issue, server QVL gap, capacity miss.  
**Target:** Accounts with active vendor-risk flag.  
**Products:** Product depends on risk.  
**Message:** Reduce platform launch risk with a qualified alternate memory path.  
**Offer:** 30-day technical proof plan and executive escalation path.  
**Assets:** Comparison battlecard, qualification proof pack, risk memo.  
**KPI:** Displacement opportunities, proof starts, share gain.
