# KPI Tracker

**Purpose:** Keep marketing accountable to revenue, not activity.

---

## Core KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Review Cadence | Target Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pipeline created | Dollar value of opportunities sourced or accelerated by memory marketing intelligence | Weekly | Rising |
| Incremental revenue | Closed revenue attributed to campaign or account action | Monthly | Rising |
| Gross margin lift | Margin improvement from mix, pricing, or LTA structure | Monthly | Positive |
| LTAs influenced | Number and value of long-term/allocation agreements influenced | Monthly | Rising |
| Design-win opportunities | New qualification/design-in opportunities opened | Weekly | Rising |
| Share gain | Account/product share movement versus prior baseline | Quarterly | Rising |
| Sales cycle acceleration | Time saved from intelligence-driven targeting | Monthly | Shorter |
| Attach rate | HBM account also buying DDR5, enterprise SSD, or CXL | Monthly | Rising |

## 90-Day Pruning Rules

- Kill a campaign if it creates no qualified meetings, no pipeline, and no roadmap engagement within 90 days.
- Downgrade a money signal if it produces interest but no account action after two review cycles.
- Keep a campaign if it creates pipeline, qualification starts, executive meetings, pricing leverage, or roadmap lock-in.
- Escalate a campaign if it produces multi-product attach potential or LTA discussion.
