2 Convergence — the customer-meeting deck

D-SAL-CUST is where all three teams meet. The Sales leader reads cross-team packs (read-only; each worker stays the single writer):

CRM-T2 understand your business  +  MI-T3 PP-T3 SM market context  +  CT-T3 roadmap fit  →  D-SAL-CUST → gap report → human leader clears every gap

Hard rule: no needs-human fact reaches a customer slide. Every number traces to a verified/reconciled source.

5 Coordination rules — what keeps it organic

  1. One writer per record. A (entity, period, metric) fact is owned by exactly one task (e.g. supply = MI-T1 or SM-T1). No double-write.
  2. Leaders are read-only across all packs, own-team and cross-team.
  3. Same contract everywhere. Every task emits the shared canonical record + trust model — that's what makes cross-team pulls clean instead of glue code.
  4. Trust travels with the fact. A record's status / source / quote ride into every downstream brief and deliverable, so a customer slide always traces to its source.
  5. Internal-gated tasks (*) emit only their public half in v1; internal facts ride as unverified-needs-human and surface in the gap report.
