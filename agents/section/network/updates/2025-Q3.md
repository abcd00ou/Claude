### 2026 — Dell'Oro Projects Ethernet to Surpass InfiniBand in AI Back-End Networks by 2027

> Dell'Oro Group projects that Ethernet will surpass InfiniBand in AI back-end network deployments by 2027. This projection reflects the growing adoption of Spectrum-X and standard Ethernet switch ASICs (Broadcom Tomahawk 5) in AI clusters, driven by lower cost and open ecosystem preferences at scale. InfiniBand remains dominant in 2025–2026 for AI training clusters.

**Source:** Network World, 2026 (citing Dell'Oro Group data)

### 2025-06-11 — Ultra Ethernet Consortium UEC 1.0 Specification Formally Released

> The Ultra Ethernet Consortium formally released the UEC 1.0 specification on June 11, 2025. The specification is 560+ pages covering a vertically integrated networking stack for AI and HPC workloads, addressing RDMA performance, congestion control, and multicast requirements. UEC 2026 technical priorities include Programmable Congestion Management (PCM). No major hyperscaler (AWS, Azure, GCP, Meta) confirmed production deployment as of the release date.

**Source:** Ultra Ethernet Consortium press release, 2025-06-11 (ultraethernet.org)

### 2025 — NVIDIA XDR 800G InfiniBand: Stargate (64K GB200) + Oracle (131K GB200) Confirmed Deployments

> NVIDIA Quantum-X800 XDR InfiniBand switches (800 Gb/s) are confirmed in production deployments: (1) Stargate AI Data Center (OpenAI/Oracle/SoftBank joint venture) — 64,000 GB200 systems installed beginning March 2025, interconnected via 800 Gbps InfiniBand XDR; (2) Oracle Zetta-scale Supercluster — 131,000 GB200 GPUs planned on Quantum InfiniBand XDR fabric. These represent two of the first confirmed large-scale XDR deployments globally. InfiniBand market revenue projected at $25.7B in 2025, rising to $127B by 2030.

**Source:** Introl Blog, 2025 (citing market reporting); NVIDIA Documentation (docs.nvidia.com), 2025
