### Update: 2026-03-16 — NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics switches announced: 100 Tb/s and 400 Tb/s configurations; CPO reduces power 3.5×

> NVIDIA announced Spectrum-X Photonics co-packaged optics (CPO) Ethernet switches for AI factories targeting millions of GPUs. Configurations: (1) 128 ports × 800 Gb/s = 100 Tb/s total bandwidth; (2) 512 ports × 800 Gb/s = 400 Tb/s. Quantum-X Photonics InfiniBand: 144 ports × 800 Gb/s, liquid-cooled. CPO integration reduces power consumption by up to 3.5× and improves resiliency by 10× versus pluggable optics. Spectrum-X surpassed a $10 billion annualized revenue run rate; InfiniBand revenue nearly doubled sequentially.

**Source:** NVIDIA Announces Spectrum-X Photonics Co-Packaged Optics Networking Switches, NVIDIA Newsroom, 2026-03-16, https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-spectrum-x-co-packaged-optics-networking-switches-ai-factories
