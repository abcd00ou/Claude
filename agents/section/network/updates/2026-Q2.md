### Update: 2026-03-16 — NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics switches announced: 100 Tb/s and 400 Tb/s configurations; CPO reduces power 3.5×

> NVIDIA announced Spectrum-X Photonics co-packaged optics (CPO) Ethernet switches for AI factories targeting millions of GPUs. Configurations: (1) 128 ports × 800 Gb/s = 100 Tb/s total bandwidth; (2) 512 ports × 800 Gb/s = 400 Tb/s. Quantum-X Photonics InfiniBand: 144 ports × 800 Gb/s, liquid-cooled. CPO integration reduces power consumption by up to 3.5× and improves resiliency by 10× versus pluggable optics. Spectrum-X surpassed a $10 billion annualized revenue run rate; InfiniBand revenue nearly doubled sequentially.

**Source:** NVIDIA Announces Spectrum-X Photonics Co-Packaged Optics Networking Switches, NVIDIA Newsroom, 2026-03-16, https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-spectrum-x-co-packaged-optics-networking-switches-ai-factories

### Update: 2026-01 — Ethernet overtakes InfiniBand: >2/3 of AI cluster switch sales in full-year 2025 (Dell'Oro Group)

> Dell'Oro Group reported that Ethernet switch sales in AI back-end networks more than doubled InfiniBand for full-year 2025. Ethernet accounted for more than two-thirds of data center switch sales in AI clusters in Q3 and Q4 2025. Two years prior (2023), InfiniBand held approximately 80% of AI back-end switch sales. Dell'Oro names Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Oracle, and xAI among hyperscalers adopting Ethernet for AI clusters. 2026 will be the first year of volume deployments of 1.6 Tbps switches, with 1600G shipments expected in H2 2026, driving additional Ethernet share gains.

**Source:** Ethernet More Than Doubles Size of InfiniBand as the Leading Fabric for AI Scale-Out Networks in 2025, Dell'Oro Group, 2026-01, https://www.delloro.com/news/ethernet-more-than-doubles-size-of-infiniband-as-the-leading-fabric-for-ai-scale-out-networks-in-2025/

### Update: 2026-03-31 — NVIDIA invests $2B in Marvell as part of NVLink Fusion ecosystem strategy

> NVIDIA invested $2 billion in Marvell in March 2026 as part of the NVLink Fusion ecosystem strategy. Marvell builds custom silicon for Amazon (Trainium), Microsoft (Maia AI accelerator), Meta (DPU), and Google (Axion ARM CPU). NVIDIA's strategic rationale: ensure its interconnect remains embedded in hyperscaler infrastructure even when hyperscalers build their own compute silicon. Arm joined the NVLink Fusion program in November 2025, enabling Arm licensees to build CPUs with native NVLink connectivity.

**Source:** The $2 Billion Nvidia Deal With Marvell Is About a Lot More Than NVLink Fusion, NextPlatform, 2026-03-31, https://www.nextplatform.com/connect/2026/03/31/the-2-billion-nvidia-deal-with-marvell-is-about-a-lot-more-than-nvlink-fusion/5213790
