## Overview

Arista Networks is the leading provider of Ethernet-based data center switching for hyperscalers and cloud providers. Its EOS (Extensible Operating System) software stack differentiates it from white-box competitors. Arista is a primary beneficiary of the Ethernet-vs-InfiniBand competition for AI cluster fabrics, with its 7800R4 series targeting AI spine/leaf topology. Arista relies on Broadcom Tomahawk/Jericho ASICs and TSMC for manufacturing.

## Supply Chain Position

Arista is fabless for switching ASICs, sourcing exclusively from Broadcom (Tomahawk 5, Jericho3). Manufacturing is through contract manufacturers (Foxconn, Celestica). Optical modules sourced from Coherent, Lumentum, II-VI.
