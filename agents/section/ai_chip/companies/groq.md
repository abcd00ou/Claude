# Groq — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Custom ASIC (LPU — Language Processing Unit)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

Groq designed the Language Processing Unit (LPU), a deterministic streaming processor optimized for low-latency AI inference. Groq operated GroqCloud for external inference API access. In December 2025, Groq entered a non-exclusive licensing deal with NVIDIA, with approximately 80% of the engineering team joining NVIDIA.
