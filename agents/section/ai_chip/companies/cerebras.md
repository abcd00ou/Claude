# Cerebras Systems — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Custom ASIC (Wafer-Scale Engine)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

Cerebras builds wafer-scale AI accelerators — single-die chips spanning an entire 300mm wafer. The CS-3 system uses the third-generation Wafer-Scale Engine (WSE-3). Primary customers include UAE's G42 and Mohamed bin Zayed University of AI.

## WSE-3 Specs

- Process: TSMC 5nm
- Die size: Full 300mm wafer
- Transistors: 4 trillion
- AI cores: 900,000
- Peak performance: 125 PFLOPS (claimed)
- Power: 23 kW per CS-3 system
