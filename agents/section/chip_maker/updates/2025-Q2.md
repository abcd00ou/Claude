## Entries (Newest First)

### 2026-01 — NVIDIA Vera Rubin Announced at CES 2026; Full Specs Disclosed

> NVIDIA announced the Vera Rubin GPU architecture at CES 2026 (January 2026). Vera Rubin R100 specifications: 336 billion transistors; TSMC 3nm dual-die design; 288 GB HBM4 per GPU; 22 TB/s memory bandwidth (vs. Blackwell B200: 8 TB/s); 50 PFLOPS NVFP4 inference per GPU (5× Blackwell inference performance). Vera Rubin NVL72 system: 72 GPUs connected via NVLink 6; 3.6 exaflops compute; 260 TB/s all-to-all bandwidth. Jensen Huang confirmed the first Vera Rubin NVL72 rack is operational at Microsoft Azure. Full production shipments targeted H2 2026.

**Source:** NVIDIA Newsroom, 2026-01; Tom's Hardware, 2026-01; ServeTheHome, 2026-01; NVIDIA Technical Blog, 2026-01
