### Update: 2026-05-11 — U.S. transformer lead times extend to four years in some segments; up to 50% of 2026 planned data centers delayed

> A pv magazine USA report dated May 11, 2026 states U.S. transformer market faces severe supply constraints as lead times extend to four years in some segments. Wood Mackenzie projects a 30% supply deficit for power transformers and 10% deficit for distribution transformers in the U.S. in 2025, worsening into 2026. Demand in 2026 is expected to exceed 2024 levels by 21% for power transformers and 16% for generator step-up units. Up to half of U.S. data centers planned for 2026 are expected to be delayed or canceled, with transformer and electrical equipment shortages cited as primary causes. Only one-third of the 12 GW of data center capacity planned for 2026 was under construction as of April 2026.

### Update: 2025-09-29 — Wolfspeed emerges from Chapter 11; eliminates $4.6B in debt; Mohawk Valley fab production continues

> Wolfspeed filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on June 30, 2025 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, citing production delays at its New York fab, increased Chinese SiC competition, flagging EV demand, and mounting debt. Wolfspeed emerged from Chapter 11 on September 29, 2025 — a 91-day prepackaged case. Approximately $4.6 billion in debt was eliminated (total debt reduced ~70%); annual cash interest expense reduced ~60%; debt maturities extended to 2030. Wolfspeed subsequently received $698.6 million in cash tax refunds from the IRS via a CHIPS and Science Act provision. Mohawk Valley fab (200mm, Marcy NY) revenue in fiscal Q1 2026: $97 million. Durham, NC 150mm device fab shut down and consolidated into Mohawk Valley ahead of schedule. SiC market share context: STMicroelectronics ~32-35% (market leader), onsemi ~25%, Infineon ~15%, Wolfspeed ~11% (2023-2025 analyst estimates).

**Source:** Wolfspeed Successfully Completes Financial Restructuring, Wolfspeed Investor Relations, 2025-09-29, https://investor.wolfspeed.com/news/news-details/2025/Wolfspeed-Successfully-Completes-Financial-Restructuring-Emerges-as-Financially-Stronger-Company-Well-Positioned-in-Silicon-Carbide-Market/default.aspx; Wolfspeed Reports Financial Results Q2 FY2026, BusinessWire, 2026-02-04, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260204660401/en/Wolfspeed-Reports-Financial-Results-for-the-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2026

### Update: 2026-03-05 — Wolfspeed launches industry-first commercially available 10 kV SiC MOSFET; targets grid, industrial, and AI datacenter power

> On March 5, 2026, Wolfspeed launched the industry's first commercially available 10 kV silicon carbide (SiC) MOSFET. Key specifications: 99% efficiency, 30% lower system cost versus incumbent technologies, 300% higher power density. Target markets: utility grid, industrial, and AI datacenter power conversion. This follows Wolfspeed's emergence from Chapter 11 on September 29, 2025. No named hyperscaler or datacenter power supplier design wins publicly disclosed post-restructuring. Wolfspeed also introduced its TOLT (top-of-line transformer) package portfolio post-restructuring: Gen 4 MOSFET-based, 650V, targeting AI datacenter rack power supplies with higher power density and top-side cooling. 800V AI datacenter SiC TAM estimated at $9–21B cumulatively 2026–2030, heavily back-loaded to 2028–2030. Mohawk Valley fab qualified for 800V architecture (relevant to NVIDIA's 800V HVDC AI datacenter standard).

### Update: 2026-01 — Wolfspeed produces industry-first single-crystal 300mm SiC wafer; AI ecosystem partners being engaged

> In January 2026, Wolfspeed produced the first-ever single-crystal 300mm silicon carbide wafer — an industry milestone with no prior public demonstration by any manufacturer. 300mm SiC wafers (vs. current 200mm standard) would enable significant die cost reduction and throughput improvements. Wolfspeed is engaging AI ecosystem partners for next-generation packaging around this milestone. No commercial timeline for 300mm SiC production has been publicly announced. This positions Wolfspeed as the technology frontier leader in SiC substrate scale, even as its current 200mm Mohawk Valley fab continues ramping.

**Source:** Wolfspeed newsroom, January 2026; Semiconductor Today, January 2026
