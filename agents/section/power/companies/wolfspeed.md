## Company Overview

Wolfspeed is the world's only fully vertically integrated 200mm SiC manufacturer, operating the Mohawk Valley fab in Marcy, NY — the largest 200mm SiC device fab globally. Following a Chapter 11 filing in June 2025 and emergence in September 2025, Wolfspeed eliminated approximately $4.6 billion in debt and refocused on Mohawk Valley. In the AI datacenter context, SiC devices address the 800V–1200V power switching required in solid-state transformers and AI rack power delivery.

## Supply Chain Position

Wolfspeed is vertically integrated: boule growth → 200mm wafer slicing and epitaxy (Mohawk Valley, Marcy NY) → device fabrication. The Durham, NC 150mm device fab was shut down ahead of schedule and consolidated into Mohawk Valley. External SiC wafer customers (STMicroelectronics, onsemi, Infineon) supplement their own crystal growth with Wolfspeed wafers.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** EV powertrain OEMs (undisclosed); STMicroelectronics (wafer supply); industrial power integrators  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Specialty SiC seed crystals (internal), process gases

## Updates

### Update: 2025-09-29 — Wolfspeed emerges from Chapter 11; eliminates $4.6B in debt; Mohawk Valley continues production

> Wolfspeed filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on June 30, 2025 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, citing production delays at its New York fab, increased Chinese SiC competition, flagging EV demand, and mounting debt. Wolfspeed emerged from Chapter 11 on September 29, 2025 — a 91-day prepackaged case. Approximately $4.6 billion in debt was eliminated (total debt reduced ~70%); annual cash interest expense reduced ~60%; debt maturities extended to 2030. Wolfspeed subsequently received $698.6 million in cash tax refunds from the IRS via a CHIPS and Science Act provision. Mohawk Valley fab (200mm, Marcy NY) revenue in fiscal Q1 2026: $97 million. Durham, NC 150mm device fab shut down and consolidated into Mohawk Valley ahead of schedule. SiC market share context: STMicroelectronics ~32–35% (market leader), onsemi ~25%, Infineon ~15%, Wolfspeed ~11% (2023–2025 analyst estimates).

**Source:** Wolfspeed Successfully Completes Financial Restructuring, Wolfspeed Investor Relations, 2025-09-29; Wolfspeed Reports Financial Results Q2 FY2026, BusinessWire, 2026-02-04

### Update: 2026-03-05 — Wolfspeed launches industry-first commercially available 10 kV SiC MOSFET; TOLT portfolio targets AI datacenter rack power

> On March 5, 2026, Wolfspeed launched the industry's first commercially available 10 kV silicon carbide MOSFET. Key specifications: 99% efficiency, 30% lower system cost versus incumbent solutions, 300% higher power density. Target markets: utility grid, industrial, and AI datacenter power conversion. Wolfspeed also introduced the TOLT (top-of-line transformer) package portfolio post-restructuring: Gen 4 MOSFET-based, 650V, targeting AI datacenter rack power supplies with higher power density and top-side cooling optimized for high-density AI server configurations. Mohawk Valley fab is qualified for 800V architecture, aligned with NVIDIA's 800V HVDC AI datacenter power standard. 800V AI datacenter SiC TAM estimated at $9–21B cumulatively 2026–2030, heavily back-loaded to 2028–2030. No named hyperscaler or datacenter power supplier orders publicly disclosed post-restructuring.

**Source:** Wolfspeed newsroom, March 5, 2026; Semiconductor Today, March 2026; Wolfspeed TOLT product launch materials

### Update: 2026-01 — Wolfspeed produces industry-first single-crystal 300mm SiC wafer; AI ecosystem partners being engaged for next-gen packaging

> In January 2026, Wolfspeed produced the first-ever single-crystal 300mm silicon carbide wafer — an industry milestone with no prior public demonstration by any manufacturer. 300mm SiC wafers (versus the current 200mm production standard at Mohawk Valley) would enable substantial die cost reduction and throughput improvement. Wolfspeed is engaging AI ecosystem partners for next-generation packaging development around this milestone. No commercial production timeline for 300mm SiC has been publicly announced. This achievement positions Wolfspeed as the substrate technology frontier leader in SiC, even as its current 200mm Mohawk Valley fab remains the only operating 200mm SiC volume production site globally.

**Source:** Wolfspeed newsroom, January 2026; Semiconductor Today, January 2026
