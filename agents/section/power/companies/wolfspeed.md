## Company Overview

Wolfspeed is the world's only fully vertically integrated 200mm SiC manufacturer, operating the Mohawk Valley fab in Marcy, NY — the largest 200mm SiC device fab globally. Following a Chapter 11 filing in June 2025 and emergence in September 2025, Wolfspeed eliminated approximately $4.6 billion in debt and refocused on Mohawk Valley. In the AI datacenter context, SiC devices address the 800V–1200V power switching required in solid-state transformers and AI rack power delivery.

## Supply Chain Position

Wolfspeed is vertically integrated: boule growth → 200mm wafer slicing and epitaxy (Mohawk Valley, Marcy NY) → device fabrication. The Durham, NC 150mm device fab was shut down ahead of schedule and consolidated into Mohawk Valley. External SiC wafer customers (STMicroelectronics, onsemi, Infineon) supplement their own crystal growth with Wolfspeed wafers.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** EV powertrain OEMs (undisclosed); STMicroelectronics (wafer supply); industrial power integrators  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Specialty SiC seed crystals (internal), process gases

## Updates

### Update: 2025-09-29 — Wolfspeed emerges from Chapter 11; eliminates $4.6B in debt; Mohawk Valley continues production

> Wolfspeed filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on June 30, 2025 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, citing production delays at its New York fab, increased Chinese SiC competition, flagging EV demand, and mounting debt. Wolfspeed emerged from Chapter 11 on September 29, 2025 — a 91-day prepackaged case. Approximately $4.6 billion in debt was eliminated (total debt reduced ~70%); annual cash interest expense reduced ~60%; debt maturities extended to 2030. Wolfspeed subsequently received $698.6 million in cash tax refunds from the IRS via a CHIPS and Science Act provision. Mohawk Valley fab (200mm, Marcy NY) revenue in fiscal Q1 2026: $97 million. Durham, NC 150mm device fab shut down and consolidated into Mohawk Valley ahead of schedule. SiC market share context: STMicroelectronics ~32–35% (market leader), onsemi ~25%, Infineon ~15%, Wolfspeed ~11% (2023–2025 analyst estimates).
