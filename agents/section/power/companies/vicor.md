## Company Overview

Vicor designs and manufactures high-density power conversion components — including Factorized Power Architecture (FPA) modules — for demanding applications requiring maximum power density in minimal space. In AI servers, Vicor's 48V direct-to-chip (D2C) components eliminate intermediate voltage conversion stages, reducing power delivery losses. Vicor's lead computing customer is Cerebras (wafer-scale engine deployments), which was in steep production ramp as of Q1 2026. Historical NVIDIA design wins include V100 SXM3 (2018) and A100 HPC systems.

## Supply Chain Position

Vicor designs proprietary power conversion topologies and manufactures modules in-house at its Andover, MA facility. It sells directly to system integrators (Cerebras, NVIDIA platform customers) and OEMs. The steep book-to-bill of >2.0 and 70% QoQ backlog surge in Q1 2026 reflects the Cerebras production ramp absorbing Vicor output.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** Cerebras (wafer-scale engine inference deployments — lead computing customer); NVIDIA (historical: V100 SXM3, A100)  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Semiconductor components (primarily from Texas Instruments, Infineon — not disclosed)
