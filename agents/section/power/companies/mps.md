**Segment:** power

## Overview

Monolithic Power Systems is the dominant supplier of voltage regulator module (VRM) ICs for NVIDIA and AMD GPU boards. Its high-current multi-phase controllers handle the sub-1V core power delivery required by advanced AI accelerators. AI server revenue is the primary growth driver, accounting for a significant and growing share of the Enterprise Data segment.

## Supply Chain Position

MPS is fabless, manufacturing at TSMC and UMC. VRM ICs are in the critical path for every AI GPU board — no GPU ships without qualified VRM. MPS holds design wins on NVIDIA H100, H200, and Blackwell-series GPU boards alongside Renesas.
