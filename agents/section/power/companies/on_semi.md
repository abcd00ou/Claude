## Overview

ON Semiconductor (onsemi) is the second-largest SiC power semiconductor supplier following Wolfspeed's Chapter 11 filing, with its EliteSiC product line gaining significant market share in 2024–2025. While EV remains the primary SiC market, data center UPS and high-density power conversion are a growing segment. Onsemi is partially vertically integrated in SiC with its Hudson Sevier substrate fab.

## Supply Chain Position

Onsemi sources SiC substrates from its Hudson Sevier facility (New Hampshire) and external suppliers. SiC devices manufactured at East Fishkill (New York) fab and Bucheon (South Korea). Internal substrate capacity provides partial independence from Wolfspeed supply disruption.
