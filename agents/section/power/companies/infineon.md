## Overview

Infineon is the world's largest power semiconductor company by revenue (~19% global share). Its CoolSiC (1200V SiC MOSFET) and CoolGaN (650V GaN) product lines address AI data center UPS, power supply, and point-of-load applications. While automotive SiC remains Infineon's largest end market, data center power is a growing segment driven by AI rack density increases.

## Supply Chain Position

Infineon manufactures SiC at its Villach (Austria) facility — the world's largest dedicated SiC wafer fab. GaN-on-Si is produced at TSMC and internal 200mm fabs. SiC substrate sourced from Wolfram, Coherent, and SiCrystal (Infineon subsidiary in Germany) — Infineon is partially vertically integrated in SiC substrate.
