# TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company)

**Segment(s):** foundry  
**Role in AI SCM:** Sole manufacturer of all leading AI chips (NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Apple); operator of CoWoS advanced packaging  
**HQ:** Hsinchu, Taiwan  
**Ticker:** NYSE:TSM / TWSE:2330  
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-16

---

## Company Overview

TSMC is the world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry and the single most critical node in the AI supply chain. Every leading AI accelerator — NVIDIA Blackwell/Vera Rubin, AMD Instinct, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Apple Neural Engine — is manufactured at TSMC. Advanced packaging (CoWoS) for AI chips is also produced by TSMC and represents a separate capacity bottleneck from wafer fabrication.

**Source:** TSMC Q1 2026 Earnings Press Release, SEC 6-K (accession 0001046179-26-000199), 2026-04-16; TSMC Annual Report 20-F (accession filed 2026-04-16)

## Supply Chain Position

TSMC is a pure-play foundry — it manufactures chips for customers but does not design chips. Its primary AI customers include NVIDIA, AMD, Google (TPU), Amazon (Trainium), Apple, Qualcomm, and Broadcom. CoWoS advanced packaging is manufactured at TSMC and is a separate, tighter capacity constraint than wafer fab.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA (confirmed CoWoS user per NVIDIA 10-K), Apple, AMD, Google, Amazon (implied)  
**Key suppliers:** ASML (EUV tools), Applied Materials, Tokyo Electron (process equipment); Ajinomoto (ABF film for substrates)

**Source:** TSMC 20-F FY2025, SEC, 2026-04-16; NVIDIA 10-K FY2026, SEC, 2026-02-25
