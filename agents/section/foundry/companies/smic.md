# SMIC — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Foundry (China domestic advanced node)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

SMIC (Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation) is China's leading foundry and the primary domestic source of advanced-node chips for Huawei and Chinese AI companies. SMIC uses modified DUV tools (ASML NXT:2000i multi-patterning) to achieve 7nm-equivalent nodes, bypassing EUV restrictions.

## Updates

> SMIC 7nm capacity: approximately 20,000 wafers per month, with Huawei absorbing approximately 15,000 WPM. Yield improved to 60–70% (from <40% at launch). SMIC plans to double 7nm capacity in 2026. 5nm pilot runs underway as of 2025; mass production targeted 2026 for Huawei Ascend AI chips and Alibaba next-generation AI processors.
