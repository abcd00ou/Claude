## Company Overview

SMIC (Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation) is China's most advanced domestic foundry and a critical component of China's AI self-sufficiency strategy. Without access to ASML EUV lithography (restricted from China since 2023), SMIC achieves 7nm-equivalent nodes using multi-patterning techniques with older DUV tools — at higher cost, lower throughput, and more limited density vs. TSMC/Samsung. In 2026, SMIC is doubling 7nm capacity and piloting 5nm for Huawei Ascend AI chips. SMIC is state-backed and strategically insulated from standard commercial market pressure.

## Supply Chain Position

SMIC's advanced node production is almost entirely directed at Huawei's HiSilicon division (AI, mobile) and Chinese cloud AI chip designers. SMIC is blocked from acquiring EUV lithography and the latest generation of etch/deposition tools (U.S. export controls, October 2022 and 2023 updates). This limits SMIC's advanced node competitiveness to DUV multi-patterning workarounds.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** Huawei HiSilicon (~75% of 7nm capacity), Alibaba Cloud DAMO, other Chinese fabless companies  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** ASML (pre-restriction DUV tools — NXT:2000i), domestic equipment (AMEC, NAURA), specialty chemicals

## Updates

### Update: 2025-08-29 — 7nm capacity ~20K WPM with Huawei absorbing 75%; doubling in 2026; 5nm pilot targeting mass production 2026

> SMIC 7nm capacity: approximately 20,000 wafers per month, with Huawei absorbing approximately 15,000 WPM. Yield improved to 60–70% (from <40% at launch). SMIC plans to double 7nm capacity in 2026. 5nm pilot runs underway as of 2025; mass production targeted 2026 for Huawei Ascend AI chips and Alibaba next-generation AI processors.
