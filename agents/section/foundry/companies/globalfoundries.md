## Company Overview

GlobalFoundries (GFS) is the third-largest semiconductor foundry by revenue, specializing in differentiated process technologies (RF, photonics, embedded memory). GFS does not compete at leading-edge nodes (5nm/3nm) — its focus is on mature nodes (12nm and above) with specialty capabilities. In 2026, silicon photonics for AI networking is the primary AI-adjacent growth driver.
