## Company Overview

GlobalFoundries is the third-largest semiconductor foundry by revenue, specializing in differentiated process technologies including RF, silicon photonics (SiPh), embedded memory, and mature logic. GFS does not compete at leading-edge nodes (5nm/3nm) — its focus is specialty nodes (12nm and above) where it is often the sole-source for specific applications. In 2026, silicon photonics revenue is targeted to roughly double, with $1B annual run rate targeted by end-2028. GF SCALE — its first OCI MSA-compliant co-packaged optics platform — was launched in May 2026.

## Supply Chain Position

GlobalFoundries supplies processed silicon wafers with integrated photonic or specialty circuit elements to fabless chip design companies (networking ASIC makers, RF chipmakers) and system OEMs that design co-packaged optics chiplets. In silicon photonics specifically, GF provides the fabrication for photonic integrated circuits (PICs) that are assembled into AI datacenter optical transceiver ASICs.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** AMD (some 12nm products), Qualcomm (some RF products), Cisco (photonics) — partially disclosed; GF rarely discloses full customer lists  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Specialty gases, 200mm and 300mm wafer suppliers

**Source:** GFS Q1 2026 earnings; GFS Annual Report
