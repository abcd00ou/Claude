## Overview

TSMC is the single most critical node in the AI supply chain. Every leading AI chip — NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Apple — is fabricated at TSMC. Advanced packaging (CoWoS, SoIC) is more supply-limited than the wafer fab process itself and is the binding constraint for AI chip shipments. TSMC Q1 2026 revenue reached $35.8B (+35.1% YoY), with HPC representing 61% of revenue. TSMC's CY2026 capex budget of $52–56B is the largest in the company's history.
