## Market Overview

TSMC is the single most critical node in the AI supply chain. Every leading AI chip —
NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Apple — is fabricated at TSMC.

Advanced packaging (CoWoS, SoIC) is the binding constraint for AI chip production in 2025–2026.
It is more supply-limited than the wafer fab process itself.

## CoWoS Capacity — Critical Bottleneck

**CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate):** TSMC's advanced packaging technology that
integrates GPU die + HBM stacks on a single silicon interposer.

- Every NVIDIA H/B-series GPU requires CoWoS-L (large format)
- Every GB200 NVL72 rack requires 72 CoWoS assemblies
- TSMC CoWoS capacity: ~25,000–35,000 wafer-equivalents/month (2025 estimate)
- Demand exceeds supply by estimated 20–30% in 2025

**CoWoS variants:**
| Variant | Interposer | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| CoWoS-S | Silicon interposer | Standard; NVIDIA H100 |
| CoWoS-L | Larger silicon | NVIDIA B-series, HBM3E |
| CoWoS-R | RDL organic | Lower cost; less bandwidth |

## SoIC (System on Integrated Chips)

TSMC's 3D stacking technology for die-to-die integration:
- **SoIC-X:** Face-to-face bonding (highest bandwidth)
- **SoIC-P:** Face-to-back bonding
- Used in: Apple M-series, future AI chips needing memory-logic integration

## Geopolitical Risk

- **Taiwan risk:** TSMC manufactures >90% of leading-edge AI chips; geopolitical concentration risk
- **TSMC Arizona fab:** N4 production started 2025; N2 planned 2028; not a near-term substitute
- **Japan fab (JASM):** N12/N16 mature nodes; not AI-relevant
- **Germany fab (ESMC):** Automotive focus; not AI-relevant
- **US CHIPS Act:** Incentivizes domestic production but 5–7 year timeline
