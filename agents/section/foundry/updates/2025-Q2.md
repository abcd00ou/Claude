## Entries (Newest First)

### 2026-01-30 — Samsung SF2P Hits 70% Yield; AMD in Final Negotiations for Venice EPYC

> Samsung confirmed that SF2P (2nm GAA) reached the 70% yield threshold required for stable mass production, following Q4 2025 earnings (reported January 30, 2026). Separately, AMD is reportedly in final negotiations (late January 2026) to use SF2P for "Venice" EPYC server CPUs, with "Verano" — an AMD agentic AI inference variant — also targeted for SF2 in 2027. This would be the first confirmed major semiconductor customer for Samsung Foundry's 2nm node beyond Preferred Networks.

**Source:** TokenRing / Wedbush investor note, 2026-01-30; AnySlicon, 2026; WCCFTech, 2026

### 2025-07-10 — Preferred Networks (Japanese AI Co.) Confirmed as First Samsung SF2 Customer

> Preferred Networks, a Japanese AI company, was confirmed as Samsung Foundry's first 2nm (SF2) customer in July 2024 (reported and confirmed via TrendForce July 10, 2024). Preferred Networks is using SF2 for custom AI chip production. This remains the only publicly confirmed AI chip customer at Samsung SF2 as of Q2 2025; AMD and Qualcomm discussions are in negotiation phase.

**Source:** TrendForce, 2024-07-10

### 2026-01 — NVIDIA Vera Rubin Confirms TSMC 3nm Dual-Die for Next-Gen AI GPU

> NVIDIA announced Vera Rubin (R100) at CES 2026 on TSMC 3nm (dual-die design). This confirms TSMC N3 as the manufacturing node for NVIDIA's 2026 GPU generation (alongside Google TPU 8 and Amazon Trainium3 on TSMC N3P). NVIDIA's CoWoS dependency for the NVL72 system (72 GPUs per rack via NVLink 6) is explicit in the architecture design.

**Source:** NVIDIA Newsroom, 2026-01; Tom's Hardware, 2026-01

### 2025 — TSMC CoWoS Capacity Doubling: 75K wpm (2025) → 120–130K wpm (end 2026)

> TSMC CoWoS-S/L capacity is projected at approximately 75,000 wafers per month in 2025, rising to approximately 120,000–130,000 wafers per month by end of 2026, representing roughly a 2× capacity increase from 2024 levels. NVIDIA secures over 60% of total CoWoS output for 2025 and 2026. CoWoS remains oversubscribed through at least 2026 despite expansion.

**Source:** FinancialContent / TokenRing analysis, 2026-01; fusionww.com; intuitionlabs.ai
