# Foundry Segment — Q1 2025 Update Log

**Period:** Q1 2025 (January–March 2025)  
**Updated:** 2026-05-12 (Cycle 4)  
**Sources:** Intel Q1 2025 Earnings (2025-04-25); TSMC Q1 2026 Earnings (2026-04-17); TechInsights (2024-Q3)

---

## Summary

Q1 2025 confirmed the structural bifurcation of the advanced foundry market: TSMC holds an effective monopoly on AI GPU manufacturing, Samsung Foundry continues to lag on 3nm yield, and Intel Foundry remains in a costly investment phase with no external AI GPU customer. CoWoS packaging demand continues to outstrip TSMC capacity by 20–30%.

### 2025-Q1 — Samsung Foundry: Yield Gap vs TSMC Persists; No AI GPU Win

> Samsung Foundry SF3E (3nm GAA) yield estimated at 35–60% vs TSMC N3 at 70%+, per TechInsights analysis published in 2024-Q3. As of Q1 2025, no NVIDIA, AMD, or Google TPU volume production at Samsung Foundry has been confirmed. Samsung's DS division reported operating losses for the Foundry segment in 2023–2024.

### 2025-Q1 — TSMC CoWoS: Demand Exceeds Capacity by 20–30%; No Near-Term Relief

> TSMC confirmed in Q1 2026 earnings (covering the period) that CoWoS advanced packaging demand continues to exceed available capacity by approximately 20–30%. TSMC's CY2026 capex of $52–56B includes significant CoWoS expansion, but analysts note relief is not expected before 2026 capex lands. Amkor being explored as potential CoWoS overflow partner (no volume confirmed).

**Source:** TSMC Q1 2026 Earnings, 2026-04-17; Industry analysis, Q1 2025
