### Update: 2026-04 — Google Ironwood confirmed on TSMC N3P; Broadcom is design and packaging partner; CoWoS packaging confirmed

> Google Ironwood (TPU v7) is fabricated on TSMC's N3P (3nm-class) process node — not N2. The chip uses CoWoS advanced packaging. Broadcom is confirmed as the chip's design and packaging coordination partner. MediaTek reportedly requested a sevenfold increase in CoWoS capacity from TSMC to fulfill Google's Ironwood orders. Ironwood is a two-chiplet design: each chiplet contains one TensorCore, two SparseCores, and 96GB HBM3E (total 192GB per chip). Google's next-generation TPU 8th generation (training chip "Sunfish" / inference chip "Zebrafish") targets TSMC 2nm, planned for late 2027 — not Ironwood.

### Update: 2026-04-16 — TSMC Q1 2026 earnings: revenue $35.9B +35% YoY; HPC/AI = 61% of revenue; gross margin 66.2%; full-year guidance above 30%

> TSMC Q1 2026 earnings (reported April 16, 2026): Revenue $35.9B USD (+35.1% YoY, +6.4% QoQ). Net profit $18.2B (+58% YoY). Gross margin 66.2% (vs 62.3% in Q1 2025). Operating margin 58.1%. CoWoS end-2026 target: 115,000–140,000 wafers/month (most-cited ~130,000 wpm vs ~35,000 wpm baseline in late 2024). End-2027 projected ~170,000 wpm. Chiayi AP7 complex is the primary CoWoS expansion site. NVIDIA, Google, Amazon, and MediaTek orders fully booked at the 140K wpm level. OSAT outsourcing: Amkor ~180–190K wafers/year; ASE/SPIL ~60–80K wafers/year.
