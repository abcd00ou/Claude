## Company Overview

Schneider Electric is a global leader in energy management and automation, supplying power distribution, UPS systems, thermal management (including liquid cooling), and the EcoStruxure DCIM platform to AI data centers. Its Systems segment — which includes Data Center solutions — is the fastest-growing division. North America is the leading regional market driven by hyperscaler AI build-out.

**Source:** Schneider Electric Q1 2026 Revenue Release, Schneider Electric IR, 2026-04-24

## Updates

### Update: 2023-01 — AVEVA Group acquisition completes at ~$10.8B; industrial software and digital twins added

> Schneider Electric completed the full acquisition of AVEVA Group plc in January 2023 for approximately $10.8-11B, delisting AVEVA from the London Stock Exchange. Schneider had previously owned approximately 60% of AVEVA since a $3.4B reverse takeover in 2017. AVEVA adds industrial software (process simulation, digital twins, MES) to Schneider's hardware and power management capabilities. AVEVA's EcoStruxure integration enables AI datacenter power and cooling management with digital twin simulation.

**Source:** AVEVA Acquisition Completion, AVEVA Press Release, 2023-01

### Update: 2026-Q1 — Systems segment (data center) +16% organic; North America +14.4%

> Schneider Electric Q1 2026 results: total revenue €9.77B (record quarterly), +11.2% organic YoY. The Systems segment (primarily data centers) grew +16% organic — the strongest segment — and represents 33% of quarterly revenues. Energy Management: +12.8% organic. North America: +14.4% organic growth, led by U.S. data center demand. Data Centers & Networks represent 30% of Schneider revenues; management targets a market CAGR exceeding 10% through 2030 driven by AI and cloud.

**Source:** Schneider Electric Q1 2026 Revenue Release, Schneider Electric, 2026; Schneider FY2025 Investor Presentation

### Update: 2026-04-24 — Q1 2026: Revenue €9.77B (+11.2% Organic); Data Center Systems +16%; Liquid Cooling Demand Highlighted

> Schneider Electric Q1 2026 revenue €9.77B (~$11.4B USD), +11.2% organic YoY, above analyst estimates. Systems segment (33% of quarterly revenue) delivered +16% organic growth, led by Data Center solutions within Energy Management. North America energy management grew +15.9% YoY, described as leading the growth due to data center build-out. Energy Management overall grew ~13% YoY. Company highlighted growing demand for liquid cooling systems for high-density AI server racks. Schneider reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance. CEO Olivier Blum stated: "The energy transition is now increasingly central to energy security and sovereignty … alongside the rapid adoption of digital and AI-enabled technologies." Liquid cooling revenue not separately quantified in press release. Middle East and Africa reported weaker performance due to regional tensions.
