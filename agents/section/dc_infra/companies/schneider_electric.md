## Company Overview

Schneider Electric is a global leader in energy management and automation, supplying power distribution, UPS systems, thermal management (including liquid cooling), and the EcoStruxure DCIM platform to AI data centers. Its Systems segment — which includes Data Center solutions — is the fastest-growing division. North America is the leading regional market driven by hyperscaler AI build-out.

## Updates

### Update: 2026-04-24 — Q1 2026: Revenue €9.77B (+11.2% Organic); Data Center Systems +16%; Liquid Cooling Demand Highlighted

> Schneider Electric Q1 2026 revenue €9.77B (~$11.4B USD), +11.2% organic YoY, above analyst estimates. Systems segment (33% of quarterly revenue) delivered +16% organic growth, led by Data Center solutions within Energy Management. North America energy management grew +15.9% YoY, described as leading the growth due to data center build-out. Energy Management overall grew ~13% YoY. Company highlighted growing demand for liquid cooling systems for high-density AI server racks. Schneider reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance. CEO Olivier Blum stated: "The energy transition is now increasingly central to energy security and sovereignty … alongside the rapid adoption of digital and AI-enabled technologies." Liquid cooling revenue not separately quantified in press release. Middle East and Africa reported weaker performance due to regional tensions.
