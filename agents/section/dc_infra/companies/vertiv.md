## Company Overview

Vertiv is the leading supplier of critical data center infrastructure: liquid cooling (direct liquid cooling units, rear-door heat exchangers, immersion), UPS systems, power distribution, and thermal management. AI data center build-out is Vertiv's primary demand driver, with AI-related orders growing significantly in 2024–2025. Vertiv's backlog and order intake are leading indicators for hyperscaler AI cluster deployment pace.

## Supply Chain Position

Vertiv designs and manufactures power and cooling equipment. It is not semiconductor-dependent for its core products (UPS, cooling systems). Key inputs are copper, steel, aluminum, and power electronics (SiC/GaN from Infineon, ON Semi, TI). Long lead-time items include power transformers (52–65 weeks) and large-format heat exchangers.
