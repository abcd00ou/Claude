## Company Overview

Eaton is a global power management company supplying critical electrical infrastructure to AI data centers: switchgear, circuit breakers, UPS systems, power distribution units (PDUs), busway, and transformers. AI hyperscaler build-out is Eaton's primary demand driver in its Electrical Americas segment. Eaton also acquired Boyd Thermal (closed March 2026), expanding into liquid cooling thermal management for high-density AI server racks.

**Source:** Eaton Q1 2026 Earnings Release, Eaton Corporation IR, 2026-05-05

## Supply Chain Position

Eaton manufactures electrical infrastructure equipment. Key raw material inputs include copper (busbars, wiring), steel (enclosures), and power semiconductors. Eaton's customers are hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, Google, Meta), colocation operators, and EPC contractors building AI data centers. Transformer supply constraint is a known industry bottleneck; Eaton is investing in capacity.
