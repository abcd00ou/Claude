## Company Overview

Hitachi Energy is a leading global supplier of power transformers, high-voltage DC (HVDC) transmission systems, grid automation software, and energy storage. In the AI datacenter context, Hitachi Energy's large power transformer manufacturing expansion in the United States is directly targeted at the supply crisis that is delaying up to 50% of planned U.S. datacenter builds in 2026. Its $457M South Boston, Virginia facility is projected to become the nation's largest large power transformer plant when completed in 2028.

## Supply Chain Position

Hitachi Energy produces large power transformers that are required for all utility-scale grid connections — including the dedicated substations that AI datacenters require. With U.S. power transformer lead times extending to 4 years in 2026 (up from 3–6 months in 2019), Hitachi Energy's U.S. manufacturing expansion directly addresses the supply-side constraint on AI datacenter construction timelines.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** U.S. electric utilities, independent power producers, hyperscaler campuses (not publicly named)  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Grain-oriented electrical steel (GOES), copper/aluminum windings, specialty insulation materials
