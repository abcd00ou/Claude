# ASML — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Semiconductor Equipment (Lithography)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

ASML is the world's sole supplier of EUV lithography systems, which are required for all advanced semiconductor nodes (7nm and below). ASML's monopoly on EUV makes its shipment schedule a hard gate on global AI chip production capacity. High-NA EUV (NXE:3800E) enables sub-2nm patterning for next-generation AI chips.
