## Company Overview

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the world's largest cloud platform and the largest declared AI infrastructure spender in 2026 at approximately $200 billion in capital expenditures. AWS operates an advanced custom AI silicon program (Trainium, Inferentia) and maintains a deep partnership with NVIDIA. As of Q1 2026, AWS has over $225 billion in revenue commitments specifically for Trainium infrastructure.

**Source:** Amazon Q1 2026 Earnings Release, SEC/q4cdn, 2026-04-29; Amazon Q1 2026 Earnings Call Transcript, Motley Fool, 2026-04-29

## Supply Chain Position

Amazon sources NVIDIA GPUs (primary AI chip partner stated on call) alongside its own Trainium ASICs. Trainium is designed in-house and manufactured at TSMC. AWS procures memory, storage, and networking at hyperscaler scale.

**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA ("deep partnership"), TSMC (implied for Trainium)  
**Key customers:** Enterprise and developer cloud customers; Trainium has $225B+ committed

**Source:** Amazon Q1 2026 Earnings Call, CEO Andy Jassy, 2026-04-29
