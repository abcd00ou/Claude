## Company Overview

Ajinomoto Fine-Techno (AFT), the electronic materials subsidiary of Ajinomoto Co., is the de facto monopoly supplier of ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) — the resin film used as interlayer dielectric in all leading-edge flip-chip BGA substrates (FC-BGA) for CPUs, GPUs, and AI ASICs. ABF is required for all advanced AI accelerator packaging (NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Intel). AI GPU substrates require 14–16 ABF layers vs. 8–12 for consumer chips, making Ajinomoto a direct gate on AI chip production capacity. In FY2026, electronic materials segment operating margin exceeded 50%.

## Supply Chain Position

Ajinomoto supplies ABF film to all major substrate manufacturers: Ibiden, Unimicron, Shinko Electric, AT&S, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, and Nan Ya PCB. These substrate makers sell FC-BGA substrates to TSMC, OSAT partners, and chipmakers. ABF price increases of 5–10% are expected in H2 2026.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** Ibiden, Unimicron, Shinko Electric, AT&S, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Nan Ya PCB  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Specialty resin and hardener suppliers (proprietary formulations), glass cloth suppliers

### Update: 2026-05-13 — 30% ABF film price hike to substrate makers effective Q3 2026; structural shortage widens to 42% by 2028; $166M capacity plan deemed insufficient

> Ajinomoto informed IC substrate makers of a 30% ABF film price increase effective Q3 2026. Standard ABF substrate prices projected to rise 5–10% in H2 2026; spot prices already up 30%+. Supply-demand shortfall projected at ~10% in H2 2026, widening to 21% in 2027 and 42% in 2028. Ajinomoto plans JPY 25 billion (~$166M USD) investment by 2030 to increase ABF capacity by 50% — analysts note this is insufficient to meet structural demand. Ajinomoto holds >95% market share for CPU/GPU ABF materials.

### Update: 2026-Q1 — ABF FC-BGA lead times extend to 28 weeks; utilization approaching 90%; supply-demand shortfall projected 21% in 2027

> Lead times for 14–16 layer ABF FC-BGA substrates (AI accelerator grade) have extended to 28 weeks, driven by bottlenecks in ABF film coating and laser-drilling equipment capacity.
