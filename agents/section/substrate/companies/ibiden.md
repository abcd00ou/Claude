# Ibiden — ABF Substrate / AI GPU Packaging

**Segment:** substrate  
**Company Slug:** ibiden  
**Last Updated:** 2025-03-01

---

## Overview

Ibiden is the second-largest ABF substrate supplier (by revenue, ~20% share) and NVIDIA's critical GPU substrate partner. AI GPU substrates have become Ibiden's dominant revenue driver, with AI-related substrates now representing over 60% of its electronics segment revenue. Ibiden's Ogaki and Fujisawa facilities are fully dedicated to high-density ABF substrates for GPU/AI chip packaging. Lead time for Ibiden substrates aligned to NVIDIA roadmap is effectively set by NVIDIA's production schedule.

## Supply Chain Position

Ibiden is the upstream packaging material supplier to TSMC's CoWoS process. GPU dies (from TSMC fab) are assembled on Ibiden FC-BGA substrates before CoWoS-L packaging. NVIDIA qualifies Ibiden as a primary substrate supplier; Shinko and Unimicron as secondary.
