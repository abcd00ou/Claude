## Company Overview

Amkor Technology is the second-largest OSAT globally by revenue and the leading beneficiary of TSMC's advanced packaging (CoWoS) outsourcing program. In 2026, Amkor receives approximately 180,000–190,000 CoWoS wafers per year from TSMC — the largest single external allocation. Amkor's Arizona campus ($7B total investment, two phases) represents the first large-scale advanced packaging facility in the United States and is being built under an MOU with TSMC to offer CoWoS and InFO packaging on U.S. soil. Advanced packaging revenue is expected to triple year-over-year in 2026.

**Source:** Amkor and TSMC Expand Partnership and Collaborate on Advanced Packaging in Arizona, Amkor Investor Relations, https://ir.amkor.com/news-releases/news-release-details/amkor-and-tsmc-expand-partnership-and-collaborate-advanced

## Supply Chain Position

Amkor sits at the back-end of the AI chip supply chain: receives CoWoS-processed wafers from TSMC (and runs its own HDFO technology), performs final assembly including solder bumping, underfill, and test, then delivers complete packaged AI accelerator modules. Amkor serves the same NVIDIA/AMD/Google customer base as TSMC CoWoS, acting as an overflow and complement to TSMC in-house packaging. The Arizona campus positions Amkor as the U.S.-based packaging option for CHIPS Act-compliant AI chip production.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA (via TSMC CoWoS outsource), AMD, Google, Amazon — supply chain reporting indicates all major AI chip customers  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** TSMC (wafer supply, CoWoS outsource), substrate suppliers (FC-BGA), underfill and encapsulant suppliers

**Source:** Amkor Q1 2026 Digitimes coverage; TSMC Q1 2026 earnings, 2026-04-16

## Updates

### Update: 2026-Q1 — Amkor-TSMC formal MOU for CoWoS and InFO in Arizona; receives ~180K–190K CoWoS wafers/year; advanced packaging to triple

> Amkor and TSMC signed an MOU to collaborate on advanced packaging in Arizona, jointly defining packaging technologies including TSMC's InFO and CoWoS to address common customers' needs. TSMC is outsourcing 240,000–270,000 CoWoS wafers annually to OSAT partners in 2026; Amkor receives the largest share (~180,000–190,000 wafers/year), with SPIL (ASE Group) receiving ~60,000–80,000 wafers/year. Amkor has two newly qualified HDFO (High-Density Fan-Out) programs for AI data centers expected to ramp in late 2026. Amkor Q1 2026 strong results; advanced packaging revenue expected to triple year-over-year in 2026.

**Source:** Amkor and TSMC Expand Partnership and Collaborate on Advanced Packaging in Arizona, Amkor Investor Relations; TSMC's CoWoS outsourcing to ASE and Amkor challenges Samsung, Digitimes, 2026-01-05; Amkor records strong 1Q26, Digitimes, 2026-04-28

### Update: 2025-10-06 — Arizona campus groundbreaking; $7B total investment; 750,000+ sq ft cleanroom; production targeted early 2028

> Amkor broke ground on its Arizona advanced packaging and test campus and expanded planned investment to $7B across two phases.
