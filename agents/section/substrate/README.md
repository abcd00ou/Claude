## Market Overview

AI chips require the most advanced substrates ever produced:
- **ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrates** — used in all high-end CPU/GPU packages
- **High-density PCBs** — AI servers require 20–24 layer boards with tight tolerances
- **Interposer substrates** — CoWoS-S and CoWoS-L require silicon interposers

Substrate supply has historically been the "forgotten" bottleneck — ABF shortage nearly
froze the semiconductor industry in 2021–2022.

## Demand Signals (AI-driven)

- GB200 NVL72 system: 72 GPU substrates + 36 NVLink switch substrates per rack
- ABF substrate content per AI server: ~$200–400 (vs $30–60 for standard server)
- AI server substrate demand growing 35–45% CAGR in 2025–2026

---

## Supply Constraints

- **ABF film itself:** Ajinomoto is sole qualified supplier of ABF film; capacity expansions take 24–36 months
- **Layer count scaling:** 24-layer AI server boards have 40% lower yields than 12-layer boards
- **Copper foil:** Ultra-low profile copper for signal integrity — limited qualified suppliers
- **Lead times:** ABF substrates for AI GPUs: 20–26 weeks; improving slowly

## Technology Roadmap

| Technology | Status | AI Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| ABF substrate (standard) | Volume | CPU/GPU packaging |
| High-density ABF (2–2–2 stack) | Ramping | AI GPU packages |
| Glass core substrate | 2025–2027 | Intel/Google exploring; better flatness |
| Panel-level packaging | R&D | Cost reduction for mid-range AI chips |
| Embedded die substrate | Emerging | Power integration in substrate |

**Glass substrate (potential disruptor):** Intel and Google are jointly developing glass core
substrates as an alternative to organic ABF. Better dimensional stability, lower loss at
high frequencies. Commercial timeline: 2027–2028.
