### Update: 2026-Q1 — ABF FC-BGA lead times extend to 28 weeks; utilization approaching 90%; supply-demand shortfall projected 21% in 2027

> Lead times for 14–16 layer ABF FC-BGA substrates (AI accelerator grade) have extended to 28 weeks, driven by bottlenecks in ABF film coating and laser-drilling equipment capacity. Demand exceeds supply capacity by more than 50% per Samsung Electro-Mechanics leadership. ABF substrate line utilization is tracking from mid-70% range (2025) to above 80% in Q1 2026, targeting 90%+ in H2 2026. Supply-demand shortfall projected at 10% in H2 2026, widening to 21% in 2027 and 42% in 2028 at current expansion plans.

### Update: 2026 — ASE CoWoP capacity targeting 20K-25K wafers/month by end 2026; ASE purchases NT$2.8B Zhunan facility; Intel EMIB evaluated by second-tier ASIC vendors
