### 2025 — Power Transformer Lead Times: 128 Weeks (2.5 Years) in U.S.; 30% Supply Shortfall

> U.S. power transformer lead times reached 128 weeks (approximately 2.5 years) in 2025, a significant worsening from the previously tracked 52–65 weeks. Wood Mackenzie reported a 30% supply shortfall for large power transformers in the U.S. in 2025, with an additional 10% shortfall for distribution transformers. Imports account for approximately 80% of U.S. power transformer supply. Demand for large power transformers is up 116% since 2019. Delays are pushing AI data center completion timelines out by 24–72 months in some cases.

**Source:** TechTicker, 2025 (citing Wood Mackenzie); Build.inc Insights, 2026; S&P Global Research, 2025

### 2025-2026 — Liquid Cooling Capacity Equals Air Cooling in 2025; Projected to Double Air by End 2026

> Liquid cooling (direct-to-chip, immersion, CDU-based) capacity equaled air cooling capacity in 2025, driven by AI clusters exceeding 120–300 kW per rack — above the practical limits of air cooling. Liquid cooling is projected to double air cooling capacity by end of 2026. DLC systems (direct liquid cooling) are now a standard component for GB200 NVL72 and equivalent AI rack systems.

### 2025-2026 — Lead Times Widening Across DC Infrastructure: Generators, MV Transformers, Cooling Systems

> Beyond power transformers (128 weeks), lead times are increasing across multiple DC infrastructure categories: diesel generators, power distribution systems, medium-voltage transformers, and advanced cooling systems are all showing lead times of "several years" in some cases. The combination of power equipment shortages and liquid cooling demand is identified as one of the primary bottlenecks constraining AI data center build-out timelines globally.
