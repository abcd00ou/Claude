### Update: 2026-Q1 — Power Transformer Lead Times: 128 Weeks Standard; 144 Weeks Generator Step-Up (as of Q2 2025 baseline)

> As of Q2 2025 (latest published data available as of May 2026): standard large power transformers average 128 weeks lead time; generator step-up transformers average 144 weeks (approximately 2.5–3 years). Hitachi Energy announced a $1B U.S. manufacturing investment including a new $457M power transformer facility in Virginia to address shortages. Between 90–125 GW of new AI data center capacity projected to be added 2025–2030, requiring up to 1,400 additional power transformers. Global transformer market projected at ~$26–27B in 2026. Standardizing equipment designs cited as a path to reduce lead times from 128 weeks to as low as 12 months.

### Update: 2026-05-11 — DLC Market: $4.07B in 2026; CAGR 31.5% to 2033; 75%+ of High-Density Servers Require Liquid Cooling

> Data center liquid cooling market estimated at $4.07B in 2026, projected to reach $27.65B by 2033 (CAGR 31.5%). Direct-to-chip (DLC cold plates) holds 42.85% of 2025 segment revenue, dominant technology. Over 75% of high-density server configurations (as reported by leading server OEMs) now require some form of liquid cooling to maintain optimal operating temperatures. Average rack density grew 69% YoY to 27 kW in 2026, driven by NVIDIA Hopper and Blackwell GPU deployments. Asia-Pacific projected to capture 42% of global DLC cold plate demand by 2026; North America leads in hyperscaler deployments.
