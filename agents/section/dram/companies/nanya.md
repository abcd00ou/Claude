## Company Overview

Nanya Technology is a Taiwan-based DRAM manufacturer and a long-standing supplier of server DRAM (DDR5 RDIMMs) and mobile DRAM. Micron holds a minority ownership stake. Nanya operates at significantly smaller scale than SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron but is drawing interest from U.S. cloud providers for DDR5 RDIMM supply diversification. In Q1 2026, Nanya reported record revenue and net income (+583% YoY), reflecting improved pricing and demand for server DRAM.

## Supply Chain Position

Nanya is a pure-play DRAM manufacturer and IDM (Integrated Device Manufacturer): it designs, fabricates, and sells DRAM. Nanya sells primarily to PC OEMs, server OEMs, and module makers. Its growing server DRAM (DDR5 RDIMM) qualification with U.S. cloud providers represents the primary AI supply chain entry point.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** U.S. cloud providers (qualification stage, not named), PC OEMs, server OEMs  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** ASML and Nikon DUV lithography tools, specialty process gases
