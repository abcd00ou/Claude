# Nanya Technology — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** DRAM  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

Nanya Technology is a Taiwan-based DRAM manufacturer, partially owned by Micron. Nanya produces DDR5 RDIMMs targeting server and AI workloads. Smaller scale than SK Hynix/Samsung/Micron but drawing interest from U.S. cloud providers for supply diversification.

## Updates
