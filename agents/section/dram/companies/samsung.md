## Company Overview

Samsung Electronics' Device Solutions (DS) Division is the world's largest semiconductor business by revenue. It includes the Memory Business (DRAM, HBM, NAND) and the Foundry Business. In Q1 2026, DS generated KRW 81.7 trillion in revenue at a 65.7% operating margin, representing approximately 94% of Samsung's total operating profit.

**Source:** Samsung Electronics Q1 2026 Earnings Conference Presentation, images.samsung.com, 2026-04-30

## Supply Chain Position

Samsung Memory sells HBM to AI accelerator OEMs (NVIDIA, AMD, and its own Exynos NPU for mobile). Server DRAM goes to hyperscalers and server ODMs. NAND products go to enterprise and consumer channels. Samsung has signed multi-year supply contracts with some customers for memory.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** Not individually disclosed; multi-year supply contracts confirmed with unnamed customers  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** ASML (EUV), equipment suppliers

**Source:** Samsung Q1 2026 Earnings Conference Call Transcript, GuruFocus, 2026-04-30

## Updates

### Update: 2025-Q2 — Samsung HBM Market Share Collapsed to 17%; Micron Overtook Samsung at #2 (21%)

> TrendForce Q2 2025 HBM market share data: SK Hynix 62%, Micron 21%, Samsung 17%. This represents a dramatic reversal from Samsung's historical #2 position. Samsung's share collapse is attributed to failure to pass NVIDIA's initial HBM3E qualification in 2024. Samsung is working to re-qualify with NVIDIA; multi-year supply contracts signed with other customers. HBM3E contract prices raised approximately 20% for 2026 delivery by Samsung and SK Hynix. Samsung's re-qualification efforts for NVIDIA HBM3E Blackwell are ongoing.

**Source:** TrendForce, 2025-12-18; Digitimes, 2025-12-24; Silicon Analysts, 2025

### Update: 2026-04-30 — Q1 2026: record revenue and profit; HBM capacity sold out for all of CY2026

> Memory Business Executive Kim Jaejune: "The adoption of agentic AI drove further growth in relevant demands, mostly for HBM, server DRAM, and server SSD. Despite rising demand, because of industry-wide constraints in expanding capacity, the supply shortage has actually become more intense."
> Samsung confirmed all HBM capacity for calendar 2026 is sold out. HBM sales are expected to more than triple year-over-year in 2026.

### Update: 2026-04-30 — Conventional DRAM more profitable than HBM recently; multi-year contracts signed

> Q&A disclosure: "Conventional DRAM has actually been delivering higher profitability than HBM recently." Management noted this is due to quarterly vs. annual pricing structures for conventional vs. HBM, and stated the margin gap is expected to narrow significantly in 2027.
> Major customers are seeking mid-to-long-term supply commitments; Samsung has signed multi-year supply contracts with some customers. Quote from Kim Jaejune: "These agreements help enhance business stability and visibility for both us and our customers."
