# CXMT (Chang Xin Memory Technologies) — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** DRAM (China domestic)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

CXMT is China's primary domestic DRAM manufacturer, state-backed, headquartered in Hefei. CXMT produces DDR5 and LPDDR5X on 16nm-equivalent process nodes using domestic and modified equipment. Represents a strategic domestic alternative to SK Hynix/Samsung/Micron within China's AI supply chain.

## Updates

### 2025-02-13 — Volume production on 16nm DDR5; 15nm R&D underway; capacity targeting 300K wafers/month in 2026

> CXMT entered volume production on 16nm DRAM (first DDR5 product). R&D on 15nm is underway; mass production targeted for late 2026. Monthly output doubled to 200,000 wafers by Q1 2025; forecast 300,000/month by 2026. Full-year 2025 output projected at 2.73M wafers (+68% YoY from 1.62M in 2024). Products: DDR5 (16Gb, 24Gb, up to 8000 MT/s) and LPDDR5X in mass production. Yield estimates vary widely (10–20% per some insiders vs. claimed ~80%).
