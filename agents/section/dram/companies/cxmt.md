## Company Overview

CXMT is China's largest domestically funded DRAM manufacturer and a key component of China's semiconductor self-sufficiency strategy. CXMT produces DDR5 and LPDDR5X using modified 16nm-class equipment (including modified ASML DUV tools acquired before restriction tightening). CXMT is not yet competitive with SK Hynix/Samsung/Micron at the leading-edge AI memory (HBM) level — CXMT does not produce HBM — but its DDR5 products are being qualified for Chinese AI accelerator and server applications. Capacity is targeted at 300,000 wafers/month by 2026.

## Supply Chain Position

CXMT sits in the China domestic DRAM supply chain, selling to Chinese AI chip customers and server OEMs (Huawei, Inspur, Sugon, Lenovo). CXMT is isolated from the global supply chain by U.S. export controls: CXMT cannot access ASML EUV (for sub-14nm nodes), newer AMAT/Lam etch tools, or U.S. memory process IP. CXMT uses older DUV multi-patterning and domestically adapted tools.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** Chinese cloud AI providers (Huawei, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu, Tencent — inferred), Chinese smartphone OEMs  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** SMIC (some wafer processing — unconfirmed), domestic equipment (SMEE, AMEC), ASML DUV (pre-restriction tools)

## Updates

### Update: 2025-02-13 — Volume production on 16nm DDR5; 15nm R&D underway; capacity targeting 300K wafers/month in 2026

> CXMT entered volume production on 16nm DRAM (first DDR5 product). R&D on 15nm is underway; mass production targeted for late 2026. Monthly output doubled to 200,000 wafers by Q1 2025; forecast 300,000/month by 2026. Full-year 2025 output projected at 2.73M wafers (+68% YoY from 1.62M in 2024). Products: DDR5 (16Gb, 24Gb, up to 8000 MT/s) and LPDDR5X in mass production. Yield estimates vary widely (10–20% per some insiders vs. claimed ~80%).
