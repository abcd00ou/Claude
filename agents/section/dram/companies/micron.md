## Company Overview

Micron Technology is the only US-headquartered manufacturer of DRAM and NAND flash memory. It holds approximately 10–15% HBM market share and is a qualified HBM supplier to NVIDIA. Micron's fiscal year ends in August; fiscal Q2 2026 covers December 2025 – February 2026.

**Source:** Micron Technology, Inc. — Q2 FY2026 Earnings Release, investors.micron.com, 2026-03-18

## Supply Chain Position

Micron manufactures DRAM at fabs in Boise (USA), Hiroshima (Japan), and Taiwan. NAND is produced in Singapore and Boise. A new Singapore NAND fab is under construction (first wafers H2 CY2028). Advanced HBM packaging is being built out at the Singapore facility, contributing from CY2027.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA (Vera Rubin HBM4 qualification confirmed)  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** ASML (EUV lithography), equipment suppliers

**Source:** Micron Q2 FY2026 Earnings Call Prepared Remarks, investors.micron.com, 2026-03-18
