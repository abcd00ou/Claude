## Conventional DRAM Pricing Cycle

### 2025 — DDR5 Server DRAM Recovery: +40–60% from 2023 Trough

> DDR5 server DRAM contract pricing recovered 40–60% from the 2023 trough as AI server deployments drove demand for DDR5 DIMMs. Server DRAM mix (DDR5 vs DDR4) shifted to DDR5 majority in 2025.

**Source:** DRAMeXchange Contract Price Monitor, TrendForce, 2025-Q4

### 2023-Q2 — DRAM Spot Price Trough: DDR5 $3.50/8Gb vs $7.50 Peak

> DRAM spot prices hit the lowest level since 2016 in Q2 2023. DDR5 spot at $3.50/8Gb equivalent; compared to peak of $7.50 in Q4 2022. All three major suppliers reported operating losses in 2022–2023.

## HBM Pricing: Allocation Mechanics

### 2025 — HBM Allocation Locked 4–6 Quarters in Advance; Spot Market Effectively Closed

> HBM is allocated exclusively through long-term supply agreements. No meaningful HBM spot market exists. Hyperscalers lock 4–6 quarter allocations directly with suppliers, meaning HBM pricing is set 12–18 months in advance.

**Source:** SK Hynix Q1 2026 Earnings Call, 2026-04-23; Micron FQ2 2026 Earnings Call, 2026-03-18
