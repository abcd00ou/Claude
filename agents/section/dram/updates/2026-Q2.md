### Update: 2026-02-12 — Samsung ships world's first commercial HBM4; specs: 3.3 TB/s, 13 Gbps I/O, 40% better power vs HBM3E

> On February 12, 2026, Samsung shipped the world's first commercial HBM4 to an unnamed customer (widely reported as NVIDIA for Vera Rubin). Product is a 12-Hi HBM4 on Samsung's 1c process node, delivering 3.3 TB/s bandwidth, 13 Gbps I/O (exceeding the 11.7 Gbps spec announced and the JEDEC spec of ~8 Gbps), and 40% better power efficiency compared to HBM3E. Samsung targets 30%+ HBM4 market share in 2026 and plans approximately 50% HBM capacity increase through 2026. Samsung's 2026 HBM3E supply is fully sold out.

**Source:** Samsung Semiconductor Newsroom Feb 12 2026; Bloomberg Feb 12 2026; TrendForce Feb 2026; KED Global Oct 2025
