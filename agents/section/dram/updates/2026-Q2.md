### Update: 2025-12 — SK Hynix announces world-first HBM4 development completion; HBM4 mass production ramp delayed from Q2 to Q3 2026

> SK Hynix completed world-first HBM4 development and readied mass production in September 2025. The original HBM4 capacity ramp plan for Q2 2026 was revised to Q3 2026 because HBM3E demand remained high enough that SK Hynix needed to maintain HBM3E manufacturing sites longer than planned. SK Hynix's M15X fab (new facility) targets first clean room completion and pilot operations in May 2026, manufacturing both HBM3E and HBM4. 16-layer HBM4 (48GB) mass production targeted Q3 2026. SK Hynix holds approximately 54% of projected 2026 HBM4 market, Samsung 28%, Micron 18% (Counterpoint Research forecast).
