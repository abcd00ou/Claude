## Summary

Q2 2025 confirmed a dramatic HBM market share realignment: SK Hynix extended its lead to 62%, Micron overtook Samsung to claim #2 at 21%, while Samsung collapsed to 17% following its failure to pass NVIDIA's initial HBM3E qualification. HBM4 mass production qualification achieved by SK Hynix in September 2025. Total HBM market projected at ~$35B in 2025, rising to ~$58B in 2026.

### 2025-Q2 — HBM Market Share Realignment: SK Hynix 62%, Micron 21%, Samsung 17%

> Q2 2025 HBM market share data (TrendForce): SK Hynix held 62% (up from ~57% in Q4 2024), Micron held 21%, Samsung fell to 17%.
