## Summary

Q2 2025 data confirms AI networking revenue acceleration across all vendors. NVIDIA InfiniBand + Spectrum-X combined networking revenue reached ~$4.6B/quarter. Broadcom confirmed AI TAM raised to $60–90B by FY2027 (which ends October 2027), inclusive of both switching ASICs and custom XPU programs. Ultra Ethernet 1.0 spec is ratified but not yet in hyperscaler production deployment. NDR InfiniBand 400G lead times improved from 20–24wk to 12–16wk.

### 2025-06-05 — Broadcom Q2 FY2025: AI Revenue $4.4B (+46% YoY); TAM Raised to $60–90B

> Broadcom Q2 FY2025 (quarter ended May 4, 2025): AI revenue $4.4B, up 46% YoY. Management raised AI serviceable addressable market (SAM) to $60–90B by FY2027, covering custom XPU programs (3 in production + 2 qualifying) and Ethernet switch ASICs. Tomahawk 5 (51.2 Tbps) deployed at multiple hyperscalers. Meta MTIA Gen 2 (2nm) design partnership confirmed.

### 2025-Q2 — Ultra Ethernet 1.0: Spec Ratified; No Hyperscaler Production Deployment Confirmed

> Ultra Ethernet Consortium (UEC) 1.0 specification ratified in 2024; as of Q2 2025, no major hyperscaler has disclosed volume production deployment of UEC-compliant hardware. UEC members include AMD, Arista, Broadcom, Cisco, HPE, Intel, Meta, and Microsoft. First silicon from UEC-member vendors expected H2 2025. InfiniBand NDR 400G remains the dominant AI training cluster interconnect.

**Source:** UEC press releases, 2023–2024; Industry analysis, Q2 2025
