### Update: 2026-03-16 — NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics and Quantum-X Photonics CPO switches announced; $4B total supplier investment

### Update: 2026-01-12 — CPO vs. pluggable timeline: mass displacement of pluggables not before 2027–2028; CPO material revenue 2028+

> Analyst consensus (IDTechEx, SemiAnalysis, FutureMarkets): CPO does not replace pluggables at scale before 2027–2028. 2026 is the qualification/limited production year for CPO. Mass displacement of pluggables is a 2028–2030 story. Celestial AI projects a $1B revenue run rate by end of CY2028, primarily from CPO pairing with Amazon Trainium 4. Broadcom's Bailly Gen 1 CPO (paired with Tomahawk) was the earliest production CPO deployment, beginning in 2023.
