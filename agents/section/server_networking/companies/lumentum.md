# Lumentum — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Optical Interconnects / Photonics

## Company Overview

Lumentum is a leading supplier of optical components (lasers, modulators) for data center transceivers and co-packaged optics. NVIDIA committed $2B to Lumentum for CPO laser supply, validating its position as a critical AI photonics supplier.
