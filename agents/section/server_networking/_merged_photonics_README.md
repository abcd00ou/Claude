## Market Overview

Optical interconnects are the bandwidth backbone of AI data centers. The 2026 transition from 400G to 800G/1.6T pluggable transceivers is driving record revenue across the sector. Co-packaged optics (CPO) — integrating optical engines directly on switch ASICs — is the next-generation architecture: NVIDIA Quantum-X Photonics (InfiniBand CPO) ships early 2026; Spectrum-X Photonics (Ethernet CPO) ships H2 2026. Mass displacement of pluggables by CPO is a 2028–2030 story.
