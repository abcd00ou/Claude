### Update: 2026-04-22 — Lam Research Q3 FY2026: $5.84B record revenue; WFE market raised to $140B; advanced packaging >50% growth

> Lam Research reported Q3 FY2026 revenue of $5.84B — record quarter. Total wafer fab equipment (WFE) market now expected at $140B in 2026, with "upside bias" and further growth in 2027. Advanced packaging revenue expected to grow more than 50% in calendar 2026, led by copper plating. Q4 FY2026 guidance: $6.60B (±$400M). ### Update: 2026-02 — Applied Materials Q1 FY2026: record DRAM revenue; NEXX acquisition for advanced packaging; 20%+ WFE growth in 2026

### Update: 2025-12-08 — Tokyo Electron targets AI-related tools at ~40% of FY2026 revenue; large-scale HBM DRAM tools ramp H2 2026

> Tokyo Electron forecasts AI-related chip equipment reaching approximately 40% of total TEL sales by FY2026 (up from ~30% in FY2025). Advanced logic for AI servers and HBM production are the primary growth engines. FY2026 revenue guidance: ¥2,600B net sales, +6.9% YoY — projected record. China share declining from ~42% (FY2024) to ~35% (FY2025); AI-driven advanced node equipment is the stated offset. Large-scale DRAM (HBM-related) equipment investments expected to begin only in H2 2026. R&D + capex commitment: ¥1.5T + ¥700B over FY2025–2029.
