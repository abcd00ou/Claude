## Company Overview

Lam Research is the world's leading supplier of plasma etch and chemical vapor deposition (CVD) / atomic layer deposition (ALD) equipment. Lam's tools are required at every advanced logic and memory node: the transition to Gate-All-Around (GAA) transistors, HBM TSV etching, and CoWoS copper redistribution layer (RDL) deposition all drive increasing Lam equipment intensity per wafer. In 2026, advanced packaging (CoWoS, HBM through-silicon via) is a major incremental revenue driver, with advanced packaging revenue expected to grow more than 50% year-over-year.

## Supply Chain Position

Lam sells equipment to TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron, Intel, and other foundries/memory makers. Lam's advanced packaging etch and deposition tools (Sabre ECD for CoWoS copper plating; TSV etch for HBM stacking) are a direct enabler of AI chip production capacity. WFE market growing to ~$140B in 2026 with "upside bias" — Lam as a ~15% share participant benefits proportionally.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron, Intel Foundry, GlobalFoundries  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Precision machined components, specialty gases (SiH4, NF3), proprietary plasmas
