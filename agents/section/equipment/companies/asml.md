## Company Overview

ASML holds a monopoly on EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) lithography systems, which are indispensable for patterning advanced semiconductor nodes used in all AI GPUs and ASICs. No foundry can manufacture leading-edge AI chips (NVIDIA Blackwell/Rubin, AMD MI450, Google Ironwood, AWS Trainium) without ASML EUV tools. High-NA EUV (NXE:3800E) enables sub-2nm patterning for the next generation of AI chips. ASML's shipment schedule is therefore a direct hard gate on the pace of global AI chip capacity expansion.

**Source:** ASML Q1 2026 Earnings Call, ASML Investor Relations, 2026-04-16, https://www.asml.com/en/investors/quarterly-results

## Supply Chain Position

ASML integrates components from >800 specialized suppliers (Carl Zeiss (optics), Cymer/TRUMPF (light sources), ASML-internal (stages)). Customers are exclusively foundries: TSMC (dominant buyer of EUV tools), Samsung Foundry, Intel Foundry, and memory makers SK Hynix and Samsung (DRAM HBM EUV adoption). U.S. export controls prevent ASML from shipping EUV tools to China; DUV NXT:2000i was restricted in 2023 and above restrictions tightened in 2024.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** TSMC, Samsung, Intel Foundry, SK Hynix, Micron  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Carl Zeiss SMT (optical columns, ~25% of ASML revenue), Cymer/TRUMPF (EUV light source), imec (R&D partner)
