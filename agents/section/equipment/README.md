## Market Overview

The semiconductor capital equipment industry supplies the tools required to manufacture AI chips at advanced nodes. Total wafer fab equipment (WFE) market raised to **$140B in 2026** (Lam Research, April 2026), with advanced packaging (CoWoS, HBM stacking) and GAA logic the key demand drivers. Equipment lead times gate AI chip output — EUV scanner delivery and advanced packaging tool availability are the critical constraints.

## Demand Signals (AI-driven)

- HBM stacking requires 3D TSV etch and deposition — structural Lam/AMAT tailwind
- GAA nanosheet (TSMC N2, Samsung SF2) requires new etch chemistry tools
- CoWoS advanced packaging uses copper plating (Lam) and metrology (KLA) tools
- Advanced packaging revenue growing >50% in 2026 at Lam Research

## Supply Constraints

- EUV Low-NA output capped at 60 units in 2026; 80 requires confirmed customer pull (ASML Q1 2026)
- High-NA (NXE:3800E) at 2 units shipped in Q1 2026; still in early adoption at TSMC/Intel
- TEL large-scale HBM DRAM equipment investments deferred to H2 2026

## Technology Roadmap

- High-NA EUV (NXE:3800E): 18nm line/space (logic), 28nm contact pitch (DRAM) single-exposure
- Advanced packaging tools: direct-bond interconnect (DBI) metrology, hybrid bonding inspection
- Gate-all-around (GAA): requires new radical treatment tools (AMAT Viva) and angstrom-level etch
