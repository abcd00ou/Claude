### 2026-04-29 — Q1 2026 Hyperscaler Capex: Combined $710–725B; Azure AI Annualized $37B; Google Cloud $20B

> Q1 2026 earnings season (April 29, 2026) confirmed combined 2026 capex guidance from Amazon + Microsoft + Google + Meta at $710–725B, up 77% versus 2025's approximately $410B. Key data points by company:
> - **Microsoft**: 2026 capex ~$190B; CFO Amy Hood attributed $25B of the increase to rising memory/component costs. Azure Q1 2026 growth: +40% YoY. Azure AI annualized revenue: $37B (+123% YoY).
> - **Alphabet (Google)**: 2026 capex guidance raised by $5B to as much as $190B. Google Cloud Q1 2026 revenue: $20B (+63% YoY), beating estimates by ~$2B.
> - **Meta**: 2026 capex raised by $10B to a range topping $145B. Cited higher memory pricing and competition for land, power, and skilled workers.
> - **Amazon**: 2026 capex projection ~$200B.

### 2025-03 — Stargate (OpenAI/Oracle/SoftBank): 64,000 GB200 Systems Installed; NVIDIA XDR InfiniBand

> The Stargate AI Data Center joint venture (OpenAI, Oracle, SoftBank) began installing 64,000 NVIDIA GB200 systems interconnected via 800 Gbps InfiniBand XDR beginning March 2025. Stargate represents one of the largest single AI infrastructure deployments globally and is a primary driver of NVIDIA Blackwell demand. This also confirms NVIDIA XDR 800G InfiniBand as the networking fabric for the world's largest known AI training cluster.
