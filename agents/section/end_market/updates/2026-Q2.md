### Update: 2026-02-24 — Meta and AMD strategic partnership: 6 GW of AMD MI450 GPUs over 5 years; ~$60B; Meta takes 10% AMD equity stake via warrants

> AMD and Meta announced a strategic partnership on February 24, 2026 for Meta to purchase up to 6 gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs across multiple generations, starting with custom MI450 accelerators. The first 1 GW deployment of AMD GPUs is scheduled to begin in H2 2026. Independent analyst estimates put the contract at approximately $60 billion over five years. Meta agreed to take a 10% equity stake in AMD via performance-based warrants tied to GPU shipment milestones (vesting at 1 GW–6 GW). Meta's infrastructure head was quoted stating that at $135B capex in a single year, "being dependent on a single supplier is an existential risk," confirming a deliberate multi-vendor strategy combining NVIDIA, AMD, and MTIA.

**Source:** AMD and Meta Announce Expanded Strategic Partnership, AMD Newsroom, 2026-02-24, https://www.amd.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-2-24-amd-and-meta-announce-expanded-strategic-partnersh.html; Meta to use 6GW of AMD GPUs days after expanded NVIDIA AI chip deal, CNBC, 2026-02-24, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/24/meta-to-use-6gw-of-amd-gpus-days-after-expanded-nvidia-ai-chip-deal.html

Key drivers of 2026 capex increase cited by companies include higher GPU/component pricing.
