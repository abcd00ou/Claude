## Company Overview

Alphabet is the second-largest declared AI capex spender in 2026. Google operates the world's largest fleet of custom AI accelerators (TPUs) and is now offering TPU access to external customers in their own data centers. Google Cloud's AI demand drove a $460B+ contracted backlog and +63% YoY revenue growth in Q1 2026.

**Source:** Alphabet Q1 2026 Earnings Release, SEC/q4cdn, 2026-04-29; Alphabet Q1 2026 Earnings Call Transcript, Motley Fool, 2026-04-29

## Supply Chain Position

Google designs TPUs in-house and manufactures them at TSMC. TPUs co-package with HBM (sourced from SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron). Google also buys NVIDIA GPUs for third-party workloads. It procures storage (object storage at EB scale) and networking equipment at hyperscaler scale.

**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** TSMC (TPU fabrication), HBM vendors (unnamed), NVIDIA (supplementary GPU fleet)

**Source:** Alphabet Q1 2026 Earnings Call, 2026-04-29
