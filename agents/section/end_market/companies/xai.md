## Company Overview

xAI (Elon Musk's AI company) operates the Colossus supercomputer in Memphis, TN — one of the world's largest AI training clusters. Colossus expanded from 100,000 to 230,000+ NVIDIA GPUs within months of initial deployment and is targeting 555,000 GPUs at 2 GW capacity.

## Updates

> xAI acquired a third Memphis building, expanding total site to 2 GW capacity. Target: 555,000 NVIDIA GPUs at approximately $18B cost — claimed to be the world's largest single-site AI training installation. Initial 100,000-GPU build completed in 122 days; doubled in 92 more days.
