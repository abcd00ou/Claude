## Company Overview

xAI is Elon Musk's AI research and deployment company, builder of the Grok family of LLMs and operator of the Colossus AI supercomputer in Memphis, Tennessee. Colossus is one of the world's largest single-site AI training clusters: it reached 555,000 NVIDIA GPUs (~$18B in GPU purchases) as of January 2026. xAI targets 2 GW total AI compute capacity, with a second major site ($20B+) announced in Southaven, Mississippi. The Memphis expansion faces regulatory friction after an EPA rule update cited environmental concerns about xAI's on-site power generation.

**Source:** xAI Colossus 2 GW expansion, 555K GPUs, introl.com, January 2026, https://introl.com/blog/xai-colossus-2-gigawatt-expansion-555k-gpus-january-2026

## Supply Chain Position

xAI is a major end-market buyer of NVIDIA GPUs (H100, H200, GB200/GB300 Blackwell). The Colossus cluster composition as of December 2025: 150,000 H100 + 50,000 H200 + 30,000 GB200 + additional units to 555,000 total. xAI built on-site dedicated power generation ("energy islands") to bypass public grid interconnection queues — this is the source of EPA regulatory friction in Memphis. xAI also partnered with HUMAIN (Saudi Arabia) for a 500MW+ AI facility.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** X platform users (Grok AI), enterprise API customers  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA (H100, H200, GB200, GB300 — primary GPU supplier)

**Source:** Data Center Dynamics, 2025-12; CNBC, 2026-01-16; HUMAIN partnership announcement
