## Company Overview

Meta is the fourth-largest hyperscaler AI infrastructure investor, with $125–145 billion in capital expenditures planned for calendar year 2026. Meta deploys a multi-chip AI strategy: NVIDIA (training), AMD (training supplement), and its own MTIA custom silicon (inference, co-developed with Broadcom on 2nm). Meta's Llama open-source model family drives significant internal and external AI compute demand.

**Source:** Meta Q1 2026 Earnings Release, investor.atmeta.com, 2026-04-29; Meta Q1 2026 Earnings Call Transcript, Motley Fool, 2026-04-29

Meta is a direct NVIDIA customer (training), AMD customer (training), and operates its MTIA custom silicon in partnership with Broadcom (design) and TSMC (implied foundry at 2nm). Meta also broke ground on a $1 billion data center in Tulsa, Oklahoma in April 2026.

**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom (MTIA co-developer)

## Updates

### Update: 2026-04-29 — Capex raised to $125–145B; higher component pricing cited; MTIA on 2nm with Broadcom

> CFO Susan Li: "We anticipate 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, to be in the range of $125–145 billion, increased from a prior range of $115–135 billion. This reflects our expectations for higher component pricing this year and, to a lesser extent, additional data center costs to support future year capacity."
> CEO Zuckerberg: "We are rolling out more than one gigawatt of our own custom silicon that we are developing with Broadcom as well as a significant amount of AMD chips to complement the new NVIDIA systems that we are rolling out as well." MTIA is co-developed with Broadcom on a 2-nanometer process and is deployed in production across recommendation model inference.
