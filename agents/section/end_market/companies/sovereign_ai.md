## Company Overview

"Sovereign AI" refers to government-backed national AI infrastructure buildouts — primarily in the Gulf region — that are emerging as the second-tier of major AI hardware buyers after U.S. hyperscalers. Key entities: (1) HUMAIN — Saudi Arabia's AI arm under the Public Investment Fund (PIF); (2) G42 — UAE's AI company backed by MGX (Abu Dhabi sovereign wealth); (3) Stargate UAE — a joint venture between OpenAI, Oracle, Cisco, NVIDIA, SoftBank, and MGX for a 1 GW AI campus in Abu Dhabi. These entities have committed over $50B in AI infrastructure investment and are purchasing NVIDIA GB300, AMD systems, and Cerebras CS-3 at scale.

**Source:** HUMAIN + NVIDIA Expanded Partnership, NVIDIA Newsroom / PR Newswire, 2025-11; Oracle/G42/Stargate UAE announcements, 2025–2026

## Supply Chain Position

Sovereign AI entities are pure buyers — end-market customers that purchase GPU clusters, networking, and datacenter infrastructure. They compete with U.S. hyperscalers for NVIDIA GB300 and Vera Rubin allocation. The U.S. government approved AI chip exports to UAE (G42) in November 2025 — a key gating event. Saudi Arabia benefits from U.S.–Saudi investment forum commitments made in 2025.

**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA (primary GPU supplier for both HUMAIN and Stargate UAE), Oracle (Stargate infrastructure), AMD (via Oracle OCI), Cerebras (G42 Condor Galaxy), AWS (Riyadh AI Zone)  
**Key customers (end users):** Saudi government, UAE government, OpenAI (Stargate UAE compute)

**Source:** NVIDIA/HUMAIN, 2025-11; Oracle Newsroom, 2025-10-14; Cerebras press, 2026-02

## Competitive Position

Sovereign AI entities are buyers, not competitors. However, they compete with U.S. hyperscalers for scarce NVIDIA GPU allocation — specifically GB300 (sold out through mid-2026) and Vera Rubin NVL72 (H2 2026 ramp). The U.S. export approval for UAE and Saudi Arabia effectively added a major new demand tier to an already constrained supply.
