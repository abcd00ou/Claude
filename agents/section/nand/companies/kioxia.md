## Company Overview

Kioxia (formerly Toshiba Memory) is the second-largest NAND flash supplier with approximately 18–20% global bit share. It operates joint fabrication facilities with Western Digital (WD) in Yokkaichi and Kitakami (Y-series fabs). Kioxia completed its Tokyo Stock Exchange IPO in December 2024, enabling independent capital expenditure decisions. Enterprise SSD for AI data centers is a growing priority alongside consumer and PC storage.

**Source:** Kioxia IPO Prospectus, Tokyo Stock Exchange, 2024-12; Kioxia FY2024 Annual Report, 2025-03

## Supply Chain Position

Kioxia and Western Digital jointly operate Y-series NAND fabs (Fab 6, Fab 7 at Yokkaichi; Fab 9 at Kitakami). Joint capacity represents ~35–38% of global NAND bit supply combined. Each partner controls its output independently but shares the fab costs proportionally. BiCS (Bit Cost Scalable) vertical NAND is the shared technology architecture.

**Key customers:** Major hyperscalers (AWS, Google, Microsoft) via ODM partners; Apple for client NAND  
**Joint venture partner:** Western Digital (WD) — independent capex but shared fabs
