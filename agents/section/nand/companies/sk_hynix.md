## Updates

### Update: 2026-04-23 — NAND ASP +mid-70% QoQ despite -10% bits; mix shift to enterprise SSD

> NAND bit shipments declined approximately 10% QoQ due to intentional product mix optimization toward higher-value enterprise SSDs. NAND ASP rose by approximately mid-70% QoQ despite the shipment decline. Q2 2026 NAND shipments forecast to increase by mid-teen percent QoQ, supported by 321-layer products and enterprise SSDs.

**Source:** SK hynix Q1 2026 Earnings Call Transcript, GuruFocus, 2026-04-23
