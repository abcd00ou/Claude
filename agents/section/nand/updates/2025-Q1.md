# Storage Segment — Q1 2025 Update Log

**Period:** Q1 2025 (January–March 2025) + February 2025 spin-off event  
**Updated:** 2026-05-12 (Cycle 4)

## Summary

Q1 2025 was defined by the Western Digital / SanDisk spin-off (completed February 19, 2025), creating two independent companies from WD's combined NAND + HDD business. Combined with Kioxia's December 2024 IPO, the NAND storage landscape now has more publicly traded, independent pure-play NAND companies. Enterprise SSD demand for AI workloads remains the primary growth driver across all NAND suppliers.

---

## Entries (Newest First)

### 2025-02-19 — Western Digital NAND Spin-Off Complete; SanDisk Corp Begins Trading

> Western Digital completed the separation of its NAND flash business as SanDisk Corporation on February 19, 2025. SanDisk (NASDAQ: SNDK) began independent trading. The Western Digital name and ticker (WDC) were retained for the HDD business. The Kioxia JV manufacturing agreement (Yokkaichi + Kitakami fabs) continues under SanDisk. Combined WD+Kioxia NAND bit output represents approximately 32–34% of global NAND bits.

**Source:** WD Separation Press Release, 2025-02-19

### 2025-02-05 — WD Q2 FY2025: Revenue $4.285B (+38% YoY); Enterprise SSD Primary Growth Driver

> Western Digital Q2 FY2025 (quarter ended January 2025): revenue $4.285B, up 38% YoY. Flash (NAND) revenue ~$2.5B; HDD ~$1.8B. Flash gross margin recovered significantly from the 2023 trough. Enterprise SSD demand for AI hyperscaler workloads cited as the strongest growth driver. Spin-off reaffirmed for February 2025.

**Source:** WD Q2 FY2025 Earnings, 2025-02-05

### 2024-12-18 — Kioxia IPO on Tokyo Stock Exchange; ~¥750B Valuation

> Kioxia Holdings completed its IPO on the Tokyo Stock Exchange on December 18, 2024, at approximately ¥750B (roughly $5B) valuation. Kioxia is the world's second-largest NAND manufacturer (~19% bit share). WD remains a JV partner for flash manufacturing at Yokkaichi and Kitakami. Post-IPO, Kioxia's BiCS 9 (300+ layer) production ramp and CD8P PCIe Gen5 SSD qualification at hyperscalers are primary strategic priorities.

**Source:** Kioxia IPO prospectus, December 2024; Tokyo Stock Exchange listing, 2024-12-18
