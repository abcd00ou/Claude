## Summary

SanDisk (post-WD spin-off, February 2025) is actively qualifying its "Stargate" QLC SSD platform at major hyperscalers — two in active qualification, third planned for 2026. Kioxia is sampling CD9P (BiCS 8-based, PCIe 5.0) and announced a 245.76 TB NVMe SSD for generative AI. BiCS 9 and BiCS 10 mass production are both being pulled forward to 2026 due to AI demand.

### 2025-Q2 — SanDisk "Stargate" QLC SSD: Two Hyperscalers in Active Qualification; Third Planned 2026

### 2025 — Kioxia BiCS 9 + BiCS 10 Mass Production Both Expedited to 2026

> Kioxia BiCS 9 (300+ layer, targeted for low-power high-performance) and BiCS 10 (332-layer) mass production are both being pulled forward to 2026, ahead of original schedules, in response to accelerating AI-driven NAND demand. BiCS 9 had been originally targeted for 2026; BiCS 10 was later. The pull-forward signals Kioxia is competing aggressively on bit-density against Samsung V9 and Micron 276-layer.

**Source:** Tom's Hardware, 2025 (citing supply chain reports)
