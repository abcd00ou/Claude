## Market Overview

AI data centers are fundamentally different from traditional cloud data centers:
- **Power density:** AI racks hit 100–200 kW/rack vs 10–15 kW for standard racks
- **Cooling:** Liquid cooling (direct-to-chip, rear-door, immersion) becoming mandatory
- **Network:** 400G/800G AI-optimized fabric required at scale
- **Build timelines:** 18–36 months from land acquisition to live cluster

Global AI data center investment: ~$200–300B in 2025–2026 combined (hyperscaler + neo-cloud).

## Demand Signals (AI-driven)

- Each GB200 NVL72 rack: ~120 kW power draw
- NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD (1,000+ GPUs): requires dedicated power substation
- Hyperscaler power procurement: Microsoft signed 10+ GW of power deals in 2024–2025
- Nuclear and natural gas deals being signed specifically for AI data centers

**Power as the binding constraint:** GPU supply is improving; power and cooling is now the critical path for 2026 cluster deployments.

---

## Supply Constraints

- **Power transformers:** 52–65 week lead times (as of 2025); shortage driven by AI + grid modernization
- **Liquid cooling units:** 40–52 week lead times for direct liquid cooling
- **Skilled labor:** Electrical engineers, data center technicians — 2–3 year shortage
- **Land with power access:** Premium locations (near hydro, nuclear, cheap grid) fully committed

---

## Technology Roadmap

| Technology | Status | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC) | Scaling now | Required for 400W+ GPU TDP |
| Rear-Door Heat Exchanger | Transitional | Retrofit for existing air-cooled DCs |
| Single-phase immersion | Early enterprise | Energy efficiency leaders |
| 2-phase immersion | R&D / pilot | Theoretical max density |
| Small Modular Reactors (SMR) | 2028–2030 | Microsoft, Google evaluating |
| HVDC power distribution | Emerging | Reduces conversion losses in AI racks |
