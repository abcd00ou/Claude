# Coherent Corp — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Optical Interconnects / Photonics  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

Coherent (formerly II-VI + Finisar) is the leading supplier of 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers and laser components for AI data centers. NVIDIA committed $2B to Coherent for co-packaged optics (CPO) component supply.
