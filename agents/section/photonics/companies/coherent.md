## Company Overview

Coherent (formed from the 2022 merger of II-VI and Finisar) is the largest global supplier of optical transceiver components and modules for AI datacenter scale-out networking. Its product portfolio spans 400G, 800G, and 1.6T optical modules and the laser/semiconductor components that go inside them. In March 2026, NVIDIA announced a $2B strategic investment commitment in Coherent with associated purchase obligations for CPO components for NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics switches. Revenue is growing rapidly: Q3 FY2026 was $1.8B, up 27% YoY.

## Supply Chain Position

Coherent is vertically integrated in photonics: it manufactures compound semiconductors (InP, GaAs, SiPh), assembles them into modules, and delivers finished optical transceivers to AI datacenter customers. Contract manufacturing partners include Fabrinet (the largest optical transceiver CM globally). Coherent's primary AI networking customers include NVIDIA (Spectrum-X), Cisco, and major hyperscalers.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA, Cisco, AWS — per Fabrinet (contract manufacturer) disclosures  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Specialty compound semiconductor substrates (InP, GaAs), optical fiber, specialty glasses

**Source:** Fabrinet Q3 FY2026 earnings (manufacturing customer disclosure); Coherent corporate filings
