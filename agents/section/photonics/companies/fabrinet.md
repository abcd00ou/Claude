## Company Overview

Fabrinet is the world's largest contract manufacturer of optical communications components and modules. Unlike component suppliers (Coherent, Lumentum), Fabrinet's role is to manufacture finished transceiver modules on behalf of optical component companies including NVIDIA, Cisco, and AWS. Its revenue trajectory is therefore a direct read on AI datacenter optical interconnect deployment pace. Q3 FY2026 revenue was $1.214B, up 39% YoY. Fabrinet is expanding capacity by approximately 50% with a new 2-million-sq-ft facility.

## Supply Chain Position

Fabrinet assembles optical transceiver modules from components supplied by its customers (NVIDIA, Cisco, AWS provide designs and component specifications). Fabrinet purchases passive components (lenses, fiber ferrules, PCBs) and provides precision manufacturing. As the primary CM for NVIDIA optical modules, Fabrinet's capacity is a direct gating factor on NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet switch deployment in AI clusters.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA, Cisco, AWS  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** PCB suppliers, precision mechanical component suppliers, passive optical components
