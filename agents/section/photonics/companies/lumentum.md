## Company Overview

Lumentum is a leading designer and manufacturer of optical and photonic products for data center and telecom networks. Its core AI supply chain role is supplying laser components — including EMLs (electro-absorption modulated lasers) and VCSELs — that go inside 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers, and the integrated photonic components required for co-packaged optics (CPO) in next-generation AI networking switches. In March 2026, NVIDIA announced a $2B strategic investment in Lumentum with multibillion-dollar purchase commitments for laser components for CPO — validating Lumentum as a strategic AI photonics supplier.

**Source:** Lumentum Q3 FY2026 Press Release, Lumentum Investor Relations, 2026-05, https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Announces-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx

## Supply Chain Position

Lumentum manufactures compound semiconductor wafers (InP, GaAs) internally at its San Jose fab, then assembles laser chips into modules and CPO components. It supplies to optical transceiver module makers and directly to NVIDIA for CPO. Contract manufacturing partner Fabrinet assembles some Lumentum designs into finished modules.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** NVIDIA (CPO $2B commitment), Cisco, major transceiver module makers  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** InP and GaAs compound semiconductor substrates, specialty packaging materials

**Source:** Lumentum Q3 FY2026 earnings; NVIDIA Newsroom, 2026-03
