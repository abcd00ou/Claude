## Company Overview

Groq designed the Language Processing Unit (LPU) — a deterministic streaming processor architecture optimized for extremely low-latency AI inference, capable of generating tokens faster than any GPU-based alternative at comparable scales. Groq operated GroqCloud as its primary revenue channel. In December 2025, Groq entered a non-exclusive licensing deal with NVIDIA worth approximately $17B in cash payments; approximately 80% of the engineering team joined NVIDIA. The remaining Groq entity continues operating GroqCloud under CEO Simon Edwards. Saudi Arabia was a major Groq customer, committing $1.5B for a 19,000+ LPU inference cluster.

**Source:** Sacra Equity Research / trade press, 2025-12-24; Groq Newsroom, 2025-02
