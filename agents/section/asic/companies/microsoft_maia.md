# Microsoft Maia 200 — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Custom ASIC  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

Microsoft's in-house AI accelerator for inference workloads, developed under Project Maia. Maia 200 is manufactured on TSMC 3nm and deployed in Microsoft's Azure AI infrastructure to power OpenAI workloads and Microsoft Foundry services.

## Key Specs (Maia 200)

- Process: TSMC 3nm, 140B+ transistors
- Memory: 216 GB HBM3e, 7 TB/s bandwidth
- On-chip SRAM: 272 MB
- Performance: >10 PFLOPS FP4, >5 PFLOPS FP8, 750W TDP
- Efficiency: 30% better performance-per-dollar vs. prior fleet hardware; 3× FP4 vs. Amazon Trainium 3

## Updates

### 2026-01-26 — Maia 200 generally available; deployed in US Central and US West 3; powers GPT-5.2

> Maia 200 is live in US Central (Des Moines, IA) and US West 3 (Phoenix, AZ). Powers GPT-5.2 (OpenAI) and Microsoft Foundry workloads. Mass production was delayed from 2025 to 2026.
