# Tenstorrent — Company Intelligence

**Segment:** Custom ASIC (Blackhole AI accelerator)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Company Overview

Tenstorrent designs RISC-V-based AI accelerators (Blackhole architecture) for cloud and edge AI. Founded by Jim Keller. Raised $693M Series D led by Samsung Securities. Galaxy Blackhole system reached general availability April 28, 2026.

## Galaxy Blackhole Specs

- Form factor: 6U chassis
- Chips: 32 Blackhole accelerators
- Memory: 1 TB GDDR6, 16 TB/s bandwidth
- Performance: 23 PFLOPS FP8
- Internal fabric: 100 Tbps aggregate Ethernet mesh
- Pricing: $110,000 per system; Galaxy Supercluster (4 systems) $440,000
