## Summary

Google Ironwood TPU (7th generation) reached general availability in November 2025. Per-chip specs: 4,614 FP8 TFLOPS, 192 GB HBM3E, 7.37 TB/s bandwidth. A 9,216-chip Ironwood pod delivers 42.5 ExaFLOPS. Amazon Trainium3 EC2 instances (Trn3 UltraServers) were announced at AWS re:Invent December 2025, running on TSMC N3P with 2.52 PFLOPS FP8 and 144 GB HBM3e per chip — 4.4× the performance of Trainium2. ### 2025-12 — Amazon Trainium3 EC2 Trn3 Announced; 2.52 PFLOPS/chip; TSMC N3P; 4.4× Trainium2

> AWS announced EC2 Trn3 UltraServer instances (Trainium3-based) at AWS re:Invent December 2025. Trainium3 specifications: 2.52 PFLOPS FP8 per chip; 144 GB HBM3e per chip; 4.9 TB/s memory bandwidth; manufactured on TSMC N3P. UltraServer config: up to 144 chips per server = 362 FP8 PFLOPs per server. Performance vs Trainium2: 4.4× performance, 3.9× memory bandwidth, 4× performance/watt improvement. Volume production phase began Q3 2025; approximately 150,000 units planned total; 80% expected complete by end of 2026, final 20% in early 2027. Trainium4 also announced with NVIDIA NVLink Fusion support; sample chips targeted Q4 2026; mass production 2027.

### 2025-11 — Google Ironwood TPU GA: 4,614 TFLOPS/chip; 192 GB HBM3E; 9,216-Chip Pod = 42.5 ExaFLOPS

> Google Ironwood (7th-generation TPU) reached general availability in November 2025 (announced "in coming weeks" per Google Cloud Blog, April 2025). Specifications: 4,614 FP8 TFLOPS per chip; 192 GB HBM3E per chip; 7.37 TB/s memory bandwidth per chip (4.5× previous generation Trillium); die size 700 mm². Pod scale: 9,216 chips = 42.5 ExaFLOPS. TSMC fabrication confirmed; specific node (N3/N4) not confirmed in research results. Note: TSMC 2nm (N2) is reported for the next generation (TPU 8t "Sunfish" / TPU 8i "Zebrafish"), targeted for late 2027.

**Source:** Google Cloud Blog, 2025-04 and 2025-11; TrendForce, 2025-11-07; The Register, 2025-11-06; The Next Web, 2025; ServeTheHome, 2025

### 2025-Q3 — Amazon Trainium3 Volume Production Begins; Strong Interest From Large and Mid-Sized Customers

> Amazon Trainium3 volume production phase began in Q3 2025, per TrendForce (October 31, 2025). TrendForce noted strong interest from both large and mid-sized enterprise customers for Trainium3-based EC2 instances, with ramp beginning in earnest in early 2026. Trainium3 is manufactured on TSMC N3P, a more advanced node than Trainium2 (N5).
