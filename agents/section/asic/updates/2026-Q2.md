### Update: 2026-04 — Google Ironwood (TPU7x) confirmed specs: 4.6 PFLOPS/chip, 192GB HBM, 7.37 TB/s, 9.6 Tb/s ICI

> Google Cloud confirmed Ironwood (seventh-generation TPU, not TPUv6) is generally available. Per-chip specs: 4.6 PFLOPS FP8, 192 GB HBM, ~7.37 TB/s memory bandwidth, 9.6 Tb/s inter-chip interconnect. A 9,216-chip superpod delivers 42.5 exaFLOPS. Ironwood delivers a 10x peak performance improvement over TPU v5p and more than 4x better performance per chip versus TPU v6e (Trillium). Projected shipment volume: 4.3M units in 2026, 10M in 2027, 35M+ in 2028.

**Source:** Ironwood TPUs and new Axion-based VMs for your AI workloads, Google Cloud Blog, 2026-04, https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/compute/ironwood-tpus-and-new-axion-based-vms-for-your-ai-workloads

### Update: 2025-12-02 — AWS Trainium4 announced at re:Invent: NVLink Fusion integration confirmed; 6x FP4 vs Trn3; ~288GB memory; late 2026/early 2027 timeline

> AWS announced Trainium4 at re:Invent on December 2, 2025. Performance specs vs. Trainium3: at least 6x FP4 processing performance, at least 3x FP8 processing performance, 4x more memory bandwidth, and approximately 2x memory capacity (reaching ~288GB). NVLink Fusion integration confirmed: AWS and NVIDIA announced Trainium4 will integrate with NVLink 6 and NVLink Fusion, allowing it to participate in NVIDIA's scale-up fabric and fit into NVIDIA MGX rack architectures. Process node not publicly disclosed. Timeline: late 2026 or early 2027.

### Update: 2026-05 — Google Ironwood (TPU v7) confirmed: TSMC N3P, 192GB HBM3E (2×96GB chiplets), 7.37 TB/s, CoWoS via MediaTek; ~1M chips planned 2026; next-gen TPU 8t "Sunfish" / 8i "Zebrafish" on TSMC N2

> Google Cloud Next 2026 confirmed Ironwood (TPU v7) specs and GA status. Process node: TSMC N3P (3nm). Memory: HBM3E, 192 GB per chip (two chiplets × 96 GB each), 7.37 TB/s bandwidth — 4.5× Trillium. Packaging: CoWoS via MediaTek-TSMC partnership (MediaTek acts as volume production coordinator). Architecture: 2 chiplets per chip, each with 1 TensorCore + 2 SparseCores. Superpod: 9,216 chips = 42.5 ExaFLOPS aggregate. Generally available on Google Cloud across US/EU/APAC regions. ~1 million Ironwood chips planned for deployment in 2026 (400K first phase via Google Cloud); up to 1 GW of Ironwood capacity accessible through Google Cloud 2026. Anthropic allocated up to 1M TPU chips + 1 GW capacity in 2026. Next gen: TPU 8t "Sunfish" (training) / TPU 8i "Zebrafish" (inference), both targeting TSMC N2, late 2027.

### Update: 2026-05 — Meta MTIA 450 specs confirmed: 7 PFLOPS FP8, 288 GB HBM, 18.4 TB/s; TSMC N2; mass deployment early 2027

> Meta confirmed MTIA 450 performance specs (March 2026 roadmap, TrendForce March 2026). MTIA 450: 7 PFLOPS FP8 compute; 288 GB HBM; 18.4 TB/s bandwidth (2× MTIA 400); 75% boost in MX4 FLOPS; hardware acceleration for FlashAttention and Softmax; targeted at GenAI inference (LLM, MoE). Process node: TSMC N2 (Broadcom described it as "industry's first 2nm AI compute accelerator"). Mass deployment early 2027. MTIA 400 targets 2026 deployment. All chips in the 400/450/500 family share the same chassis/rack/network — enabling drop-in upgrades across generations.
