### Update: 2026-04 — Google Ironwood (TPU7x) confirmed specs: 4.6 PFLOPS/chip, 192GB HBM, 7.37 TB/s, 9.6 Tb/s ICI

> Google Cloud confirmed Ironwood (seventh-generation TPU, not TPUv6) is generally available. Per-chip specs: 4.6 PFLOPS FP8, 192 GB HBM, ~7.37 TB/s memory bandwidth, 9.6 Tb/s inter-chip interconnect. A 9,216-chip superpod delivers 42.5 exaFLOPS. Ironwood delivers a 10x peak performance improvement over TPU v5p and more than 4x better performance per chip versus TPU v6e (Trillium). Projected shipment volume: 4.3M units in 2026, 10M in 2027, 35M+ in 2028.

**Source:** Ironwood TPUs and new Axion-based VMs for your AI workloads, Google Cloud Blog, 2026-04, https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/compute/ironwood-tpus-and-new-axion-based-vms-for-your-ai-workloads

### Update: 2026-03 — Meta announces four-generation MTIA roadmap (300–500); MTIA 300 in production

> Meta announced a four-generation MTIA roadmap (MTIA 300/400/450/500). MTIA 300 is already in production for ranking and recommendation training: 800W TDP, 1.2 PFLOPS FP8, 216 GB HBM. ### Update: 2025-12-02 — AWS Trainium4 announced at re:Invent: NVLink Fusion integration confirmed; 6x FP4 vs Trn3; ~288GB memory; late 2026/early 2027 timeline

> AWS announced Trainium4 at re:Invent on December 2, 2025. Performance specs vs. Trainium3: at least 6x FP4 processing performance, at least 3x FP8 processing performance, 4x more memory bandwidth, and approximately 2x memory capacity (reaching ~288GB). NVLink Fusion integration confirmed: AWS and NVIDIA announced Trainium4 will integrate with NVLink 6 and NVLink Fusion, allowing it to participate in NVIDIA's scale-up fabric and fit into NVIDIA MGX rack architectures. Process node not publicly disclosed. Timeline: late 2026 or early 2027.

**Source:** AWS Integrates AI Infrastructure with NVIDIA NVLink Fusion for Trainium4 Deployment, NVIDIA Technical Blog, 2025-12-02, https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/aws-integrates-ai-infrastructure-with-nvidia-nvlink-fusion-for-trainium4-deployment/; Amazon to fuse Nvidia's NVLink into Trainium4 accelerators, The Register, 2025-12-02, https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/02/amazon_nvidia_trainium/

### Update: 2026-03 — Meta MTIA next generation on TSMC N2 (first AI chip on 2nm); 1 GW initial deployment committed; Broadcom multi-gen co-development partnership

> Meta's next-generation MTIA chip will be the first AI chip manufactured on TSMC N2 (2nm process). Meta committed to deploying 1 gigawatt (1,000 MW) of MTIA chips initially, scaling to multiple gigawatts by 2027. Meta deepened its partnership with Broadcom to co-develop "multiple generations" of MTIA chips covering chip architecture co-development across the MTIA roadmap (MTIA 300/400/450/500). MTIA 450 is slated for H2 2026 targeting generative AI inference; MTIA 500 targeted for 2027. Meta Superintelligence Labs appointed a dedicated hardware leader to oversee custom chip development for AGI workloads.

**Source:** Expanding Meta's Custom Silicon to Power Our AI Workloads, Meta Official Blog, 2026-03, https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/expanding-metas-custom-silicon-to-power-our-ai-workloads/; Meta commits to deploying 1 gigawatt of MTIA chips, SiliconAngle, 2026-04-14, https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/14/meta-doubles-partnership-broadcom-committing-1-gigawatt-custom-ai-processors/
