## Entries (Newest First)

### 2025-11 — Infineon FY2025: AI Revenue ~€700M; Targets €1.5B FY2026, €2.5B FY2027

> Infineon FY2025 total revenue: €14.662B (-2% YoY, reflecting mixed market conditions). AI-specific revenue in FY2025 reached approximately €700M, a new revenue stream for the company. Infineon raised its AI revenue target to approximately €1.5B in FY2026 and approximately €2.5B in FY2027, with the ramp constrained by capacity rather than demand. Products driving AI revenue: CoolSiC MOSFETs (UPS and AC/DC PSU), GaN (high-density power conversion), and multi-phase VRM controllers for AI server boards.

**Source:** Infineon press release, Nov 2025; Infineon FY2025 Q4 web presentation; ProcurementPro

### 2025 — onsemi FY2025: ~$7.8B Revenue; 200mm SiC Achieved; AI DC Revenue Doubles in 2026

> onsemi FY2025 revenue: approximately $7.8B (slight contraction from FY2024 due to non-AI market weakness). 2026 guidance projects toward $9B, with AI data center power revenue guided to double year-over-year in 2026. Key 2025 achievement: mass production of SiC on 200mm wafers (approximately 80% more chips per wafer vs. 150mm), a critical cost reduction. onsemi also introduced vertical GaN (vGaN) power semiconductors targeting AI cluster power delivery (VRMs and high-density DC/DC conversion). Q2 2026 gross margin guidance: 38–40%.

**Source:** onsemi press release (FY2025 results); mlq.ai; onsemi investor page
