## Summary

Ibiden announced a JPY 500B (~$3.3B) three-year capital investment starting FY2026 for IC package substrate expansion — the largest single ABF substrate investment commitment disclosed publicly. Unimicron is maintaining capex above NT$25B in 2026 (>20% above 2025). Only Ibiden and Unimicron are confirmed as having the process capability for the most advanced AI server ABF substrates; customers are increasingly concerned about supply shortfalls over the next 2–3 years.

## Entries (Newest First)

### 2026-04-30 — Unimicron 2026 Capex NT$25B+; Targeting CoWoS and EMIB Advanced AI Substrates

> Unimicron is maintaining capital expenditure at over NT$25B (approximately US$790M) in 2026, more than 20% above 2025 capex levels. The company is specifically targeting high-end ABF substrates compatible with TSMC CoWoS and Intel EMIB advanced packaging processes. Embedded substrate competition is intensifying: Samsung, Ibiden, and Unimicron are all competing for AI GPU substrate design wins.

**Source:** Digitimes, 2026-04-30; Digitimes, 2025-12-18

### 2026-02-04 — Ibiden: JPY 500B (~$3.3B) Three-Year Investment Starting FY2026

> Ibiden announced plans to invest JPY 500B (approximately US$3.3B) over three years starting FY2026 for IC package substrate capacity expansion. This is the largest publicly disclosed ABF substrate capital commitment from any single substrate manufacturer. The investment targets AI GPU and ASIC substrate supply for TSMC CoWoS ecosystem customers. New capacity lines are expected to begin contributing in FY2027–FY2028.

**Source:** Digitimes, 2026-02-04

### 2025-Q4 — Only Ibiden + Unimicron Have Process Capability for Most Advanced AI Substrates

> Industry reports note that only Ibiden and Unimicron currently have the process capability to manufacture the most advanced AI server ABF substrates (required for NVIDIA B200/Vera Rubin, AMD MI-series, and Google TPU class packages). Customers are increasingly worried that advanced substrate capacity will fall short over the next 2–3 years. Samsung, Ibiden, and Unimicron are racing to win next-generation AI GPU substrate programs.

**Source:** Digitimes, 2026-04-30; Digitimes, 2025-12-18
