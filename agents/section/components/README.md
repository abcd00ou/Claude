## Overview

AI chips require the most advanced substrates ever produced: ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrates for GPU/CPU packaging, high-density PCBs (20–24 layer) for AI server boards, and silicon interposers for CoWoS advanced packaging. Substrate supply has historically been the "forgotten" bottleneck — ABF shortage nearly froze the semiconductor industry in 2021–2022. AI server substrate content is approximately $200–400 per server vs $30–60 for standard servers. Ajinomoto is the sole qualified supplier of ABF film with 24–36 month expansion lead times.

## Technology Roadmap

### 2027–2028 — Glass Core Substrate: Commercial Launch Target

> Glass core substrate replaces organic resin core with ultra-flat glass, enabling 2× finer routing (5μm L/S vs 10μm for ABF). Better thermal stability reduces package warpage during solder reflow. Intel/Google targeting 2027 commercial availability.

**Source:** Intel Glass Substrate Roadmap Presentation, Intel Corporation, 2024; "Next-Gen Substrate Technology," Yole Développement, 2024-Q4

#segment:substrate #source-tier:A #signal-type:roadmap #date:2024 #importance:high #confidence:high

### 2026 — Panel-Level Packaging (PLP): Cost Reduction for Mid-Range AI Chips

> Panel-level packaging (PLP) uses large rectangular panels (510×515mm) instead of round wafers (300mm), reducing substrate area cost by ~30–40%. ASE Group and Amkor are leading PLP development for mid-range AI ASICs.

**Source:** ASE Group Technology Day 2025, ASE Group, 2025; SEMI PLP Workshop Report, SEMI, 2025

#segment:substrate #source-tier:A #signal-type:roadmap #date:2025 #importance:medium #confidence:high

### 2025 — High-Density ABF (2-2-2 Stack): Standard for AI GPU Packages
