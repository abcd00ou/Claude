## Company Overview

AT&S is Austria's largest PCB and substrate manufacturer, with a growing role in AI-grade ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrates for advanced AI chip packages. Its Kulim (Malaysia) manufacturing campus is operating at 100% utilization supplying substrates to TSMC (CoWoS ecosystem) and Intel Foundry. AT&S holds approximately 10% of the high-end AI ABF substrate market by revenue, competing with Ibiden and Unimicron.

**Source:** AT&S FY2024 Annual Report (fiscal year ending March 2024); Industry market share estimates, TrendForce 2024

## Supply Chain Position

AT&S supplies ABF substrates to TSMC's advanced packaging (CoWoS) supply chain and to Intel Foundry for leading-edge chip packages. The Kulim campus in Malaysia is AT&S's primary AI substrate site; a second phase (Kulim 2) has been approved for capacity expansion. ABF resin supply is sourced from Ajinomoto (Japan), which is a single-source supplier of ABF film used industry-wide.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** TSMC (CoWoS substrate supply), Intel Foundry  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Ajinomoto (ABF film), Isola (resin systems)

**Source:** AT&S Annual Report FY2024; Industry supply chain analysis
