**Segment:** substrate

## Overview

Unimicron is the largest ABF substrate supplier by revenue (~25% global share) and a critical supplier to TSMC's CoWoS ecosystem. Its Taoyuan and Hsinchu facilities serve AMD, Intel, and TSMC advanced packaging programs. Unimicron is deeply embedded in the TSMC supply chain and benefits from any CoWoS capacity expansion.

## Supply Chain Position

Unimicron sources ABF film exclusively from Ajinomoto Fine-Techno and copper foil from Mitsui Mining and JX Nippon. Unimicron is a tier-1 substrate supplier to TSMC, delivering substrates used in CoWoS assemblies. AMD Instinct MI series GPU packages are a key Unimicron program.
