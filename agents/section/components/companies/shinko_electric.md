## Company Overview

Shinko Electric Industries is a Japanese semiconductor packaging substrate manufacturer and a subsidiary of Fujitsu Limited. It holds approximately 15% of the high-end AI ABF substrate market, serving Intel as its primary captive customer. Shinko is engaged in R&D for high-density substrates compatible with CoWoS-like advanced packaging. Unlike Ibiden and Unimicron, Shinko's revenue is heavily weighted toward Intel, creating concentration risk if Intel's foundry volumes do not grow.

**Source:** TrendForce Substrate Market Analysis, 2024; Shinko Electric FY2024 Annual Report (Japanese language)

## Supply Chain Position

Shinko Electric's substrate production is anchored by Intel's demand for CPU and GPU packaging substrates. As Intel's Gaudi AI accelerator and server CPU (Xeon) production continue, Shinko remains the primary domestic substrate partner. Shinko is investing in finer-pitch substrates to support Intel 18A and future advanced packaging. If Intel Foundry's external customer base grows, Shinko would be a direct beneficiary for substrate supply.

**Key customers (publicly stated):** Intel (primary captive); limited external customers  
**Key suppliers (publicly stated):** Ajinomoto (ABF film), Sumitomo Bakelite
