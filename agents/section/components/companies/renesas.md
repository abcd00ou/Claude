## Company Overview

Renesas Electronics is a leading analog and power management IC supplier with confirmed VRM design wins on NVIDIA GPU boards. Alongside Monolithic Power Systems (MPS), Renesas competes for multi-phase controller and power stage design wins on each NVIDIA GPU generation. AI server is Renesas's fastest-growing end market, offsetting softness in automotive and industrial.

**Source:** Renesas Q1 2025 Earnings Call, Renesas Electronics Investor Relations, 2025-04-25

## Supply Chain Position

Renesas is a mixed-signal fabless/IDM company, manufacturing at TSMC (leading-edge analog) and its own fabs (mature nodes). AI server power ICs are produced at TSMC for advanced controllers and at Renesas fabs for power stages.
