## Cross-Segment Signals

### Signal 1: Memory supply is structurally constrained through at least 2027

All three major memory suppliers independently stated that their entire CY2026 HBM supply is fully committed and that shortages will persist or worsen into 2027.

| Company | Statement | Source date |
|---|---|---|
| Micron | "Entire calendar 2026 HBM supply, including HBM4, is fully committed" | 2026-03-18 |
| Samsung | "All HBM capacity for calendar 2026 is sold out"; 2027 gap "more severe than 2026" | 2026-04-30 |
| SK Hynix | "Customer demand for the next three years already exceeds supply capacity"; "structural, not temporary" | 2026-04-23 |

**Cross-segment implication:** Full 2026 HBM commitment means NVIDIA Blackwell, AMD Instinct, and hyperscaler ASIC programs are all drawing from a fully subscribed pool. Any supply disruption (CoWoS yield issue, HBM4 ramp delay) would have immediate cascade effects on GPU shipments.

→ Cross-ref: `dram/`, `chip_maker/`, `foundry/`

### Signal 2: Hyperscaler component pricing is inflating capex — signals chip/packaging ASP pressure

Both Microsoft (+$25B) and Meta (+$10B) raised their CY2026 capex guidance specifically citing "higher component pricing." This is a top-down confirmation of the ASP increases reported bottom-up by memory suppliers (DRAM +mid-60–90% QoQ, NAND +55–75% QoQ).

| Company | Component pricing impact | Source date |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft | ~$25B of $190B CY2026 capex | 2026-04-29 |
| Meta | +$10B raise (from $115–135B to $125–145B) | 2026-04-29 |

**Cross-segment implication:** Higher component pricing in hyperscaler budgets is confirmed by memory supplier ASP increases. This is a closed loop — supply-constrained memory → higher ASP → inflated capex guidance.

→ Cross-ref: `dram/`, `storage/`, `end_market/`, `dc_infra/`

### Signal 4: NVIDIA CoWoS dependency confirmed in 10-K; bottleneck persists

NVIDIA's 10-K explicitly states: "We utilize CoWoS technology for semiconductor packaging." TSMC's 20-F confirms CoWoS as a production service. TSMC's FY2026 capex budget is $52–56B — the largest in its history — but CoWoS capacity figures are not disclosed in formal filings.

**Cross-segment implication:** With both NVIDIA (Blackwell) and AMD (Instinct series) dependent on TSMC CoWoS, and hyperscaler ASICs (Google TPU 8, Amazon Trainium3) also using TSMC advanced packaging, CoWoS remains the single tightest constraint in the AI chip supply chain.

→ Cross-ref: `foundry/`, `chip_maker/`, `asic/`

- Micron: HBM4 36GB 12-high in volume shipments (Vera Rubin confirmed)
- Samsung: HBM4 >50% of Samsung HBM sales from Q3 2026; HBM4E samples Q2 2026
- SK Hynix: HBM4 in mass production; HBM4E samples H2 2026 (1Cnm node)
- NVIDIA: Vera Rubin production H2 FY2027 — next major HBM4 demand event
- AMD: MI455X + Samsung HBM4 collaboration confirmed

**Cross-segment implication:** HBM3E remains the dominant volume node through 2026. HBM4 ramp is beginning. HBM4E defines the 2027 memory supply challenge. All three suppliers are targeting HBM4E mass production in 2027 — the same year NVIDIA Vera Rubin ramps and shortages are forecast to worsen.

→ Cross-ref: `dram/`, `chip_maker/`, `foundry/`

### Signal 6: AMD gaining hyperscaler footprint — Meta and OpenAI each commit 6 GW

AMD's MI450 received two landmark 6-gigawatt commitments: Meta (public Q1 2026 earnings) and OpenAI (10-K October 2025, confirmed). These are the largest publicly disclosed AI chip purchase agreements by watt beyond NVIDIA.

**Cross-segment implication:** AMD's CoWoS demand at TSMC increases substantially. Samsung HBM4 supply for AMD (MI455X) represents a new demand channel outside the NVIDIA-SK Hynix primary relationship. NVIDIA's stated exclusion of China DC compute from guidance leaves a strategic gap AMD may address.

→ Cross-ref: `chip_maker/`, `foundry/`, `dram/`, `end_market/`

### Signal 7: Agentic AI drives new demand shape — inferencing + recommendation > training

Multiple companies cited "agentic AI" as a new demand driver that increases inference and recommendation workloads:
- Samsung: "adoption of agentic AI drove further growth in relevant demands, mostly for HBM, server DRAM, and server SSD"
- NVIDIA CEO: "the agentic AI inflection point has arrived"
- Samsung: "Key Value SSD for AI inference workloads" — new storage product category
- Microsoft: constrained despite adding 1 GW/quarter; workloads are continuous, not batch

**Cross-segment implication:** Inference-optimized products (HBM4E, TLC KV-SSD, Instinct MI450, MTIA Gen 2) are gaining importance relative to training-only products. Storage (KV-SSD) becomes a new AI-specific market. DC power density continues to grow.
