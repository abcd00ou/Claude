## Executive Summary

The AI supply chain has bifurcated in 2026: **compute is abundant but infrastructure is the binding constraint.** Hyperscaler capex upgraded to $695–715B combined (Big-4 only), yet only 5 of 16 GW of announced US AI data center capacity is under construction — the gap is entirely attributable to power grid delays (transformer/switchgear lead times, interconnection queues), not chip availability. Three simultaneous HBM4 supplier certifications (June 5, 2026) and Samsung's HBM4E sample shipments mark the HBM4 transition as underway; packaging/substrate has replaced memory as the supply chain bottleneck. Ethernet decisively holds ~67% of AI back-end switch sales, ending InfiniBand's era of majority share. NAND entered a structural supply deficit — all major suppliers sold out for 2026.
