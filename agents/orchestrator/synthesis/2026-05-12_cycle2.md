### Signal 9: Ethernet Is Winning AI Cluster Fabric — Broadcom TAM and Arista Revenue Confirm Shift

Both Broadcom (Tomahawk 5, Jericho3-AI networking revenue ~$4B/Q) and Arista ($1.93B revenue, AI cluster fabric now "material") confirm that Ethernet-based AI cluster fabric is gaining against InfiniBand at hyperscalers. Microsoft and Meta — the two largest Arista customers — are deploying Ethernet AI fabric alongside (not replacing) NVIDIA InfiniBand, suggesting a dual-fabric architecture at scale.

**Cross-segment implication:** NVIDIA's InfiniBand monopoly in AI cluster networking is being challenged. The Ultra Ethernet Consortium (AMD, Intel, Broadcom, Google, Meta as founding members) is producing real silicon. Broadcom's $60–90B TAM estimate for AI semiconductors assumes substantial Ethernet AI fabric expansion — this is the networking equivalent of the GPU TAM expansion story.

## Structural Thesis — Full Cycle View

After 2 cycles covering all 10 segments, the AI supply chain has one structural characteristic: **TSMC is the single point of dependency across all segments.**

- DRAM: HBM logic die fabricated at TSMC
- Storage: NAND controllers at TSMC
- Foundry: All leading AI chips at TSMC
- Chip Maker: NVIDIA, AMD both at TSMC CoWoS
- ASIC: All hyperscaler ASICs at TSMC
- Network: Marvell, Broadcom switch ASICs at TSMC N5/N3
- Power: Less TSMC-dependent (Infineon, onsemi have captive fabs)
- Substrate: Ibiden/Unimicron supply TSMC's CoWoS substrate requirements

Any disruption at TSMC propagates through every segment simultaneously. This concentration is the single largest systemic risk in the AI semiconductor supply chain.
